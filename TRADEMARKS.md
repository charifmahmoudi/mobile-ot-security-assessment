# Atlas OT Scout trademark policy

The Apache License 2.0 grants copyright and patent permissions for the
software. It does not grant permission to use the Atlas OT Scout name, logos,
or other project marks in a way that implies endorsement, certification, or
official distribution.

People may accurately refer to the unmodified project as Atlas OT Scout and
may say that their product is based on Atlas OT Scout. Forks and modified
distributions should use a different product name and branding unless the
project maintainer gives written permission.

This policy is intentionally separate from the software license. It protects
the reliability signal that adopters need when evaluating official releases,
capability packages, appliance images, and assessment reports.
