# Governance

The repository owner is the final decision maker during research and design. Material decisions require an architecture decision record (ADR) and a pull request.

Maintainer responsibilities include evidence integrity, operational safety, privacy, dependency/license review, release signing and respectful review. No contributor may merge active OT behavior without a second technical review and an explicit safety review.

A commercial/open-source licensing decision is intentionally deferred. Contributions are not accepted under an implied license; establish LICENSE and contribution terms before implementation contributions.
