# Research methodology and evidence standard

## Purpose

This repository substitutes structured desk diligence, technical experiments and market proxies for unavailable pre-prototype interviews. It does not pretend that desk research proves product-market fit.

## Claim ledger

For each material claim record: claim, scope, source URL, publisher, publication date, access date, evidence grade, contradictions, inference and next test. Prefer regulators, ministries, operators, manufacturers, standards bodies and original repositories.

## Plant/vendor discipline

A company operating a factory is verified by a company or authoritative source. A vendor family supporting a protocol is verified by its manual. These two facts may **not** be joined into “factory uses vendor” without plant-specific evidence.

## Alternatives to interviews

- Public tender and procurement requirement analysis.
- Job-description keyword analysis, respecting site terms.
- OEM/integrator case studies.
- Annual reports, certification scopes and site lists.
- GitHub issue/maintenance signals for candidate libraries.
- Controlled lab benchmarks and replay corpora.
- Landing-page conversion and checklist download tests.
- Fixed-price assessment offers and letters of intent.
- Distributor/integrator requests for quotation.
- Import-schema tests using public/sample inventories.

These create stronger evidence than invented survey numbers but still do not replace production-user observation.

## Contact research

Maintain account-level information in the public repository. Store personal leads only in an access-controlled CRM with source, date, lawful basis, do-not-contact status and retention. Use publicly posted corporate email/contact forms. Never guess email patterns, scrape LinkedIn, publish personal emails or imply endorsement.

## Statistical rules

- State numerator, denominator and sample construction.
- Label desk samples as samples, not population estimates.
- Do not report precision unsupported by the source.
- Preserve dated source snapshots/metadata where licensing permits.
- Recalculate metrics from machine-readable tables.
