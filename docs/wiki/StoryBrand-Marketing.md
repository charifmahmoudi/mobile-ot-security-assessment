# StoryBrand and marketing

The original StoryBrand draft was withdrawn because it inferred customer emotions, buying triggers and promised outcomes without customer evidence.

Use the [evidence-grounded StoryBrand](../diligence/STORYBRAND-GTM-PLAN.md).

Current message:

> Atlas OT Scout is an open research effort to build a controlled mobile evidence instrument for industrial audit teams.

Current call to action:

> Review the evidence specification.

Stronger product and emotional language is prohibited until supported by lab results, written method review, observed use or purchase behavior.
