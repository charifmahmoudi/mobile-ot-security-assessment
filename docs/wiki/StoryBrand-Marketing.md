# StoryBrand marketing plan

## Brand script

**Character:** a plant engineer, industrial integrator or assessor responsible for uptime but asked to prove what is connected.

**External problem:** brownfield assets span wired, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth networks; inventories are incomplete and tools are fragmented or expensive.

**Internal problem:** the customer lacks confidence that an assessment is complete and fears disrupting production.

**Philosophical problem:** teams should not need enterprise-scale infrastructure simply to obtain a trustworthy OT baseline.

**Guide:** Atlas OT Scout brings protocol-aware safety, offline evidence and a repeatable field workflow. Credibility must come from transparent coverage, lab results, signed rules and local partners—not “AI magic.”

**Plan:**
1. Define scope and attach the approved interface/TAP.
2. Observe first; run only approved identity queries.
3. Review evidence, prioritize gaps and export the inventory.

**Direct call to action:** “Run a controlled site baseline.”  
**Transitional call:** “Download the OT field-readiness checklist.”

**Success:** a defensible asset baseline, clear unknowns, repeatable evidence and a prioritized action list.  
**Failure:** hidden assets, spreadsheet drift, inconsistent contractor work and costly emergency discovery.

## Message hierarchy

- Headline: **Know what is connected—without bringing a server into the plant.**
- Subhead: **Offline-first OT/IoT discovery across Ethernet, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, designed to observe before it asks.**
- Proof: protocol coverage matrix, safety case, lab replay corpus, export examples and measured pilot results.
- CTA: **Plan a baseline assessment.**

Avoid unprovable phrases such as “complete visibility,” “zero risk,” “AI-powered pentest” or “all OT vendors.”

## Channels

- Industrial automation integrators and electrical contractors.
- Moroccan cybersecurity MSSPs and audit firms.
- Sector associations and industrial-zone operators.
- Engineering schools and controlled OT labs.
- French/Arabic LinkedIn thought leadership directed to roles, not scraped individuals.
- Demonstrations at industrial, cyber and maintenance events.
- Procurement-ready one-page safety case and data-handling annex.

## Content sequence

1. “Why a USB Ethernet adapter is not automatically a packet TAP.”
2. Morocco sector asset/protocol hypothesis map with evidence grades.
3. Safe versus unsafe OT discovery matrix.
4. Offline evidence and data-sovereignty walkthrough.
5. An anonymized lab benchmark.
6. Integrator margin/service blueprint.
7. Buyer checklist mapped to NIST 800-82 and DGSSI direction.

## Low-cost-country expansion

Score countries using industrial concentration, Android/field mobility, local integrator density, cybersecurity/regulatory momentum, import/appliance friction, language localization, payment/procurement fit and safe-support capacity. Expand through a validated local partner and a country evidence pack; do not assume Morocco's protocols, laws or pricing transfer unchanged.
