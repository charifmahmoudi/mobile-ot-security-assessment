## Verified industrial anchors

- Renault identifies Tangier and SOMACA/Casablanca as Moroccan production sites; SOMACA expected nearly 100,000 vehicles in 2024 and exports two-thirds of output.
- Stellantis announced the Kenitra site's expansion toward 400,000 vehicles/year plus electric-mobility objects.
- OCP documents mining, Jorf Lasfar and Safi industrial operations; Jorf Lasfar alone is an integrated plant-nutrition platform.
- Managem reports 13 mining operations and 20 industrial units across Africa and lists six Moroccan operating mines.
- Safran reports more than 4,800 staff and ten Moroccan sites.
- Morocco's Ministry of Industry names major agro-food operators including Cosumar, Centrale Danone, COPAG, Nestlé, Mondelez, Lesieur Cristal and others.
- Cosumar reports mills in five regions plus a Casablanca refinery.
- Tanger Med Zones reports 1,500 companies and 145,000 jobs.

## Buying-center model

A Moroccan industrial account commonly requires several approvals. Do not treat an employee list as an organization chart.

| Role | Interest | Likely objection | Evidence to bring |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plant/operations director | uptime and audit readiness | scanner may stop production | safety case, passive mode, lab results |
| Automation/maintenance lead | accurate asset identity | generic IT findings are noise | device/protocol coverage and confidence |
| CISO/IT security | governance and consistency | unmanaged field tool | scope control, logs, encryption, MDM |
| Procurement | price, vendor continuity | small vendor risk | support model, escrow/export, partner |
| Legal/DPO | data handling | packet capture may include personal data | minimization, retention and Law 09-08 mapping |
| Integrator/MSSP | margin and repeatability | channel conflict | partner edition and deal registration |

## Account research policy

Public Git documentation records company-level sources, public corporate contact routes and likely **roles**, not private dossiers. Individual LinkedIn profiles may be reviewed during legitimate account planning, subject to platform terms, but personal email guesses and scraped contact databases must not be committed here. Verify current title, consent, lawful basis and corporate contact route in the CRM.

## Evidence gaps

No public source located in this research pass proves the installed PLC/DCS vendor at most named Moroccan plants. The protocol catalog is therefore a **coverage plan**, not an installed-base claim. Validation routes include public tenders, OEM case studies, job descriptions, integrator references, lab devices and authorized site observations.
