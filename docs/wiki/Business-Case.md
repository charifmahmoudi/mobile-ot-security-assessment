# Business case

## Why Morocco is a credible beachhead

Morocco has both concentrated industrial ecosystems and a cost/skills gap suited to a portable field product. The Ministry of Industry's 2024 barometer reports **MAD 898 billion industrial revenue, MAD 842 billion production, MAD 90 billion investment and 1,038,133 industrial jobs**. Automotive alone represented MAD 196 billion and roughly 250,000 direct jobs. Tanger Med Zones reports 1,500 companies, 145,000 jobs and a 3,000-hectare industrial platform. These are dense channels for integrators and assessors, not merely isolated end customers.

The regulatory direction reinforces the operational need: Morocco has a National Cybersecurity Strategy 2030, Law 05-20 on cybersecurity, and Law 09-08 for personal-data protection. The commercial argument is therefore not “fear of hacking”; it is affordable evidence for inventory, risk governance, supplier assurance and incident readiness.

## The economic wedge

Permanent OT monitoring platforms are valuable but require architecture, sensors, integration and recurring budget. A phone-based instrument addresses a different moment:

- pre-sales and site survey;
- brownfield inventory baseline;
- multi-site spot checks;
- incident triage;
- contractor handover validation;
- evidence collection before a larger platform purchase.

It can become a feeder to incumbent platforms rather than competing head-on.

## Buyer value model

Use customer inputs rather than unsupported ROI claims:

```
Annual value =
  assessments/year × hours saved × loaded hourly rate
+ avoided travel/appliance deployment
+ accelerated remediation value
+ attach revenue for MSSP/integrator
- subscription - field kit - training
```

A pilot must measure setup time, assets/hour, false identifications, manual reconciliation time and report-production time. No ROI percentage should be marketed before these measurements exist.

## Reasons to buy

1. **Works where cloud does not:** offline processing reduces connectivity dependency and data-export concerns.
2. **Uses equipment teams already carry:** Android plus an approved USB-C Ethernet adapter and optional TAP.
3. **Safety is a product control:** passive default, per-protocol query budgets, allowlists, stop controls and immutable logs.
4. **Evidence travels with the finding:** each asset claim points to observations, packet hashes and rule versions.
5. **Local fit:** French/English workflow, Moroccan regulatory mapping, local integrator channel and price architecture.
6. **Interoperable:** import existing inventories and export normalized results; avoid creating another silo.

## Commercial model hypothesis

| Offer | Customer | Scope | Pricing principle |
|---|---|---|---|
| Community | labs/education | passive inventory, limited packs | free/open core candidates |
| Professional | assessor/integrator | signed packs, reporting, projects | per named practitioner/year |
| Team | plant/MSSP | shared cases, governance, connectors | workspace + seats |
| Enterprise | multi-site/regulated | private packs, support, deployment controls | annual contract |
| Field kit | all | approved NIC, cables, optional TAP, case | low-margin standardized hardware |

Prices remain a design hypothesis until willingness-to-pay evidence is obtained. A procurement-friendly pilot can be sold as a fixed-scope service before software licensing is finalized.

## Competition

| Category | Examples | Strength | Gap Atlas addresses |
|---|---|---|---|
| Enterprise OT visibility | Claroty, Dragos, Nozomi | continuous monitoring and mature analytics | deployment and cost can exceed a field survey need |
| General network tools | Nmap, Wireshark | broad and familiar | not OT-safe workflow, inventory provenance or mobile product |
| Android utilities | PCAPdroid, WiFiAnalyzer-class apps | mobility and low cost | fragmented evidence; limited OT semantics |
| Open OT stacks | Malcolm/ICSNPP, Zeek packages | deep passive analysis | server/appliance operational footprint |
| Autonomous pentest platforms | PentAGI | orchestration and reporting concepts | unsafe product boundary for production OT; desktop/container assumptions |

## Decision gates

- **Gate 1 — evidence:** lab corpus proves safe identity methods for priority protocols.
- **Gate 2 — desirability proxy:** paid or signed design-partner pilot commitments, tender evidence, or distributor demand; interviews are not the only signal.
- **Gate 3 — feasibility:** USB Ethernet behavior, capture modes and Android OEM matrix pass.
- **Gate 4 — compliance:** threat model, legal review, licenses and privacy design approved.
- **Gate 5 — commercialization:** measured pilot economics and support burden justify pricing.
