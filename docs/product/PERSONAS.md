# Evidence-backed product personas

_Reviewed: 2 September 2026._

These personas are **role archetypes, not biographies of named prospects**. They are derived from public professional roles, projects and responsibilities recorded in the [Morocco prospect intelligence directory](../business-development/PROSPECT-DIRECTORY.md), combined with the workflow and uncertainty documented in the [commercial diligence summary](../business-development/DILIGENCE-SUMMARY.md).

A named individual is evidence that a role, responsibility or project context exists. Their inclusion here does **not** mean that Atlas knows their private motivations, frustrations, current tools, budget, buying intent or preference for a mobile workflow.

## Evidence boundary

Each persona separates three levels of knowledge:

- **Observed context** — supported by public role, project, procurement, professional activity or the normative P0 workflow.
- **Product-relevant job** — a role-level job inferred from multiple observed contexts and the assessment method. This guides product design but still requires practitioner validation.
- **Hypothesis to validate** — an unproven claim about pain, preference, workflow improvement, adoption or commercial value. A hypothesis must not be rewritten elsewhere as a customer fact.

The [prospect directory](../business-development/PROSPECT-DIRECTORY.md) remains the authority for named people and current public evidence. This page owns the product archetypes derived from that evidence.

## P-01 — industrial audit / OT-security practitioner

**Archetype.** A qualified industrial-audit practitioner, OT-security consultant or methodology owner working across authorized customer engagements.

**Evidence anchors.** The prospect directory identifies OT/cybersecurity and methodology roles at DATAPROTECT, NearSecure and TECHSO GROUP, including ICS/OT consulting, cyber-resilience leadership, critical-infrastructure work and technical executive responsibility. The diligence identifies qualified industrial-audit providers as a high-value first validation/channel set because they can judge evidence quality and methodological fit.

**Observed context.** These practitioners work in environments where authorization, methods, tools, evidence quality, customer data handling and professional conclusions matter.

**Product-relevant job.** Produce defensible OT evidence under an authorized method, preserve provenance, distinguish observation from inference, avoid unsafe collection and hand results into an existing assessment workflow.

**Decisions Atlas must support.**

- Is this collection method permitted and sufficiently bounded?
- What exactly supports an identity claim or finding?
- Is the evidence reproducible and reviewable?
- What remains unknown because of visibility, scope or evidence quality?
- Can the finalized material be handed into an existing professional method without reconstructing its provenance?

**Hypotheses to validate.**

- Atlas reduces reconciliation or report-preparation effort compared with the practitioner's current toolchain.
- The evidence schema and audit trail fit an accepted industrial-audit methodology.
- A dedicated offline appliance is operationally preferable in at least some engagements.
- The practitioner would use Atlas repeatedly and can justify its acquisition/support cost.

## P-02 — water project / commissioning engineer

**Archetype.** A project, commissioning, completion or handover engineer responsible for moving a water/wastewater system from design/construction into accepted operation.

**Evidence anchors.** Current public roles include ONEE water/desalination project managers, OCP Green Water wastewater and industrial-development program leaders, SRM sanitation-project leadership and JESA completion/commissioning and process-engineering practitioners. The prospect directory records examples including Youssef Bensaid, Faissal El Hanouch, Abdessamad Chatabi, Mohammed Amine El Farah, Abdessamad GHACHA, Said Hajjami and related engineering roles.

**Observed context.** These roles operate across project records, contractors, equipment, commissioning evidence, acceptance and handoff to an operator.

**Product-relevant job.** Determine whether the installed/observed state is consistent with the expected equipment baseline and make unresolved differences explicit before responsibility is handed over.

**Decisions Atlas must support.**

- Which expected assets have adequate field evidence?
- Which observed endpoints are unexpected or not reconciled?
- Which identity attributes conflict across sources?
- Is the available capture/field evidence sufficient for the acceptance question?
- What exceptions must remain open at handover?

**Hypotheses to validate.**

- Field-to-record reconciliation consumes material engineering time today.
- Evidence collected once with provenance can reduce handover rework or repeat visits.
- Atlas can fit FAT/SAT/commissioning documentation without adding unacceptable process overhead.

## P-03 — water operations / maintenance owner

**Archetype.** A water or wastewater operations, exploitation, provincial-network or maintenance leader responsible for continuity and the actual field state of distributed assets.

**Evidence anchors.** The prospect directory records operations/maintenance contexts including OCP Green Water industrial operations, SRM provincial utility operations and SRM water exploitation/maintenance activity. Examples include Abousselham Otmane, Souhail Soujaa and Smail ES SELLAMI.

**Observed context.** These roles deal with operational continuity, distributed infrastructure, maintenance, pumping/treatment equipment and incomplete or changing field information.

**Product-relevant job.** Establish a bounded, trustworthy field-evidence snapshot without creating unacceptable production risk, especially before maintenance, troubleshooting, acceptance of inherited infrastructure or investigation of an asset discrepancy.

**Decisions Atlas must support.**

- Is the proposed assessment safe for the operating condition and window?
- What assets or communication evidence were actually observed in this bounded area?
- What was not observed and why can absence not be assumed?
- Which discrepancy needs maintenance, engineering or asset-record follow-up?
- When should an active action be stopped immediately?

**Hypotheses to validate.**

- A portable evidence workflow reduces equipment burden, reconciliation effort or repeat field visits.
- Operations teams will permit the supported appliance and collection modes under site policy.
- The resulting evidence is useful enough to change a maintenance or operational decision.

## P-04 — GIS / patrimony / asset-information owner

**Archetype.** A GIS, patrimony, geomatics, asset-information or related data owner responsible for the accepted representation of field infrastructure.

**Evidence anchors.** The prospect directory identifies current GIS/patrimony and asset-information roles at several SRMs, including public signals around Hamza FILALI, Serhane Rachid, Ayoub Azzouzi and institutional GIS programs.

**Observed context.** These roles maintain or improve authoritative records while field evidence can be incomplete, conflicting, stale or produced by different projects and operators.

**Product-relevant job.** Decide whether a field observation should change the accepted asset record while preserving the evidence and reasoning behind that decision.

**Decisions Atlas must support.**

- Is an observation strong enough to modify an accepted record?
- Which expected and observed records are probable matches rather than confirmed matches?
- What conflicting identity/location information must remain unresolved?
- What provenance must be retained when an accepted record changes?
- Which field exceptions should return to engineering or operations rather than be silently merged?

**Hypotheses to validate.**

- Provenance-backed reconciliation is materially better than the account's current process.
- Atlas exports can fit an existing GIS/CMMS/asset-data workflow without becoming another isolated inventory.
- The organization will accept Atlas observations as proposed evidence rather than as an authoritative source by default.

## P-05 — OT / security approver

**Archetype.** An RSSI, OT-security specialist or security-governance owner responsible for deciding whether an assessment tool may operate and how collected evidence may be handled.

**Evidence anchors.** The prospect directory identifies security roles such as the SRM Casablanca-Settat RSSI and OT-security practitioners in engineering/audit organizations. The P0 contract separately requires a security approver for interfaces, active targets, retention and export constraints.

**Observed context.** This role controls or influences authorization, network boundaries, data handling, appliance policy and evidence export in sensitive environments.

**Product-relevant job.** Authorize only the minimum network and data authority required for a defined assessment question, and verify afterward that Atlas stayed inside that authority.

**Decisions Atlas must support.**

- What interface, target, operation and time window is being requested?
- Does the method transmit traffic, and what exact network effect is permitted?
- What data will be retained/exported and for how long?
- Can local stop authority terminate activity without network access?
- Is there evidence that active execution remained within the approved scope?

**Hypotheses to validate.**

- The offline and bounded architecture removes meaningful approval barriers compared with existing tools.
- Security teams accept the execution evidence and data model as sufficient for governance.
- Mobile/dedicated-appliance policy permits Atlas on target sites.

## P-06 — independent reviewer / methodology owner

**Archetype.** A senior assessor, audit-methodology owner or independent reviewer who did not make every original observation and must challenge the professional conclusions before finalization.

**Evidence anchors.** The industrial-audit channel demonstrates organizations with methodology and technical leadership, while the P0 contract and assessment method require an independent reviewer to accept/reject material identity claims and findings before finalization.

**Observed context.** Professional assessment quality depends on distinguishing evidence from interpretation and preventing weak or contradicted claims from silently entering the final report.

**Product-relevant job.** Review material claims against their original evidence, confidence, limitations and analyst decision history before allowing them into the finalized professional deliverable.

**Decisions Atlas must support.**

- Is this identity sufficiently supported by independent evidence?
- Does a conflict or weak identifier require the claim to remain unresolved?
- Is this finding reportable under the method's quality gate?
- Are visibility and sampling limitations stated accurately?
- Does the final package reproduce the reviewed state exactly?

**Hypotheses to validate.**

- The evidence graph and review model reduce reviewer effort while improving defensibility.
- Reviewers can understand Atlas evidence without relying on the original collector's memory.
- The final package is usable inside existing customer/audit QA processes.

## P-07 — evaluation sponsor

**Archetype.** A director, program leader or executive who can authorize a bounded evaluation and decide whether Atlas merits further operational or procurement attention.

**Evidence anchors.** The prospect directory identifies executive and program sponsors across ONEE, OCP Green Water, SRMs, engineering firms and audit providers. These roles establish authority and organizational context; they do not establish personal interest in Atlas.

**Observed context.** A sponsor allocates attention/resources and needs a defined evaluation question rather than an open-ended technology demonstration.

**Product-relevant job.** Decide whether a bounded Atlas evaluation produced enough operational/professional value and acceptable risk to justify continuation, procurement or a larger trial.

**Decisions Atlas must support.**

- What exact operational/professional question did the evaluation attempt to answer?
- What evidence or decisions did Atlas improve?
- What limitations or deployment blockers remain?
- What measurable effort, equipment or rework changed during the evaluation?
- Is there a justified next step rather than a generic request for broader deployment?

**Hypotheses to validate.**

- Atlas creates measurable value in field preparation, reconciliation, evidence QA or reporting.
- The value is large enough to support a paid evaluation or acquisition.
- The organization has a workable procurement/support route for the appliance and related services.

## Supporting system role — pack administrator

The P0 contract also defines a **pack administrator** who installs approved signed content packs without changing finalized cases. This is an important security/operations role, but current business-development evidence does not justify treating it as a primary buyer/user persona. Stories involving pack administration should therefore be driven by the normative security/product contract rather than invented customer behavior.

## Validation discipline

Persona confidence should increase only from observed behavior: practitioner review, controlled evaluation, interview, procurement outcome or measured workflow data. For each evaluation, capture at least:

- current tools and handoffs;
- preparation and field time;
- reconciliation/review/report effort;
- repeat visits or rework;
- evidence/schema acceptance;
- site/mobile/data-policy blockers;
- who actually approves technical use and purchase;
- willingness to continue, pay or procure.

When observed customer behavior contradicts a persona hypothesis, update the persona and stories rather than rationalizing the discrepancy.
