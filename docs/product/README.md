# Product design

This section translates the P0-WATER specification and commercial/practitioner evidence into user outcomes, role archetypes, interactions and demonstrations.

- [Evidence-backed personas](PERSONAS.md) — role archetypes derived from public prospect/workflow evidence, with observed context separated from unvalidated product and commercial hypotheses.
- [User stories](USER-STORIES.md) — persona-driven professional decisions and acceptance outcomes across prepare, authorize, collect, reconcile, review, handoff and evaluation journeys.
- [Open-source and UX implementation](OPEN-SOURCE-AND-UX-IMPLEMENTATION.md) — design system, implementation choices, reusable components and remaining gates.
- [Presenter demo script](DEMO-SCRIPT.md) — live product walkthrough for a coherent assessment story.

The persona layer intentionally consumes the [business-development prospect intelligence](../business-development/PROSPECT-DIRECTORY.md) without turning named people into fictional biographies. Current named-account/person research remains owned by business development; product design owns the role archetypes and hypotheses derived from that evidence.

The composed prospect video and its provenance are maintained separately in the [demo section](../demo/README.md).
