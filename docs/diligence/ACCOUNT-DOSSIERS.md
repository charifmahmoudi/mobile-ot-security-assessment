# Water-utility account dossier framework

Use this framework for ONEE Water Branch and Morocco's regional multiservice companies in their water and sanitation scope.

## Required account facts

1. Legal operator and asset owner.
2. Province, site and process-area boundary.
3. Water or wastewater service responsibility.
4. Current project, maintenance or acceptance event.
5. Operations, automation, maintenance, security and procurement roles.
6. Official contact or procurement route.
7. Known evidence source: inventory, drawing, capture or contractor handover.
8. Explicit unknowns and claims that require primary confirmation.

## Qualification score

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Water-sector fit | Not water | Mixed/unclear | Explicit water or wastewater |
| Bounded pilot | Regional estate only | Candidate area | One named area |
| Evidence access | None | Possible | Approved artifact or lab |
| Technical owner | Unknown | Role identified | Named and confirmed |
| Safety acceptance | Broad scanning expected | Undecided | Passive-first accepted |
| Project timing | No event | General modernization | Current handover/maintenance project |

Do not promote an account to pilot status below 9/12 or when water-sector fit, bounded pilot or safety acceptance scores zero.
