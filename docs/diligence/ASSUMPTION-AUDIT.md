## Surviving evidence-backed claims

- Morocco has substantial industrial activity.
- DGSSI recognizes industrial-systems security audit as a qualification domain.
- applicable sensitive systems are subject to qualified periodic audit requirements.
- named Moroccan public bodies procure automata maintenance, telemanagement, VTS, surveillance, electrical and network work.
- named Moroccan employers seek PLC/SCADA and multiple industrial-protocol skills.
- Android supports USB host and BLE APIs, subject to device/permission constraints.
- switched Ethernet visibility requires correct network placement such as TAP/SPAN.
- open-source protocol libraries exist, with material license and safety constraints.

## Rule

A sentence may appear in product marketing only if it has:

1. a source or measured product result;
2. a scope that matches the evidence;
3. no unstated causal link;
4. a review date;
5. a clear owner for revalidation.

Normative design principles must be labeled as principles. Future capabilities must be labeled as targets. Unknowns must not be converted into customer quotations or emotions.
