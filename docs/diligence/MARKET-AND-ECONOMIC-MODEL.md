# Observable market evidence and economic unknowns

## Conclusion

A defensible TAM, SAM or SOM cannot currently be calculated from public evidence. The previous numeric scenarios have been withdrawn.

No source located provides all three required inputs:

1. number of Moroccan industrial sites requiring this workflow;
2. purchase incidence or assessment frequency;
3. accepted annual or per-engagement price.

A large industrial-company count is not equivalent to a software market.

## Economic measurements required

### Per assessment

- authorized preparation hours;
- equipment and travel cost;
- capture/collection time;
- number of assets requiring manual reconciliation;
- report production hours;
- repeat visit or rework;
- product-support hours;
- value of reusable evidence across future audits.

### Per buyer

- assessments per year;
- number of practitioners;
- procurement method and budget owner;
- existing tools and licenses;
- acceptable deployment model;
- accepted offer and payment term;
- renewal condition.

## Pricing experiment

Use three quotes, not a guessed pricing page:

1. fixed-price evidence-processing service using imported PCAP;
2. controlled field-kit evaluation through a qualified auditor/integrator;
3. annual practitioner license only after repeated use.

Record issued price, scope, response, objections, procurement stage and outcome. A price becomes evidence only when accepted, rejected with reason, or compared in a real procurement process.

## Market-sizing release condition

Publish TAM/SAM/SOM only when the repository contains:

- a deduplicated site census for at least the priority clusters;
- a documented inclusion rule;
- assessment frequency from procurement or operating evidence;
- at least five real price observations;
- a reachable-buyer/channel rate;
- explicit overlap and sensitivity treatment.

Current market size: **unknown**.
