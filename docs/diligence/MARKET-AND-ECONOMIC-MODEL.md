# Market, pricing and economic model

## Evidence discipline

No authoritative public count of “Moroccan sites willing to buy mobile OT discovery” was found. Therefore TAM/SAM/SOM below are **decision scenarios**, not market facts. The repository exposes every assumption so it can be replaced.

## Market anchors

- 2024 Moroccan industry: MAD 898bn revenue, MAD 842bn production, MAD 240bn value added, MAD 90bn investment and 1,038,133 jobs (Ministry of Industry).
- Automotive: MAD 196bn revenue and about 250,000 direct jobs (same barometer).
- Tanger Med industrial platform: 1,500 companies and 145,000 jobs.
- Aerospace: about 150 firms, $2.6bn exports and 17,000 direct jobs as of December 2024 (US Commercial Service).
- Tanger Med's sector page cites 2,100 agribusiness companies nationally; this is an ecosystem count, not an OT-buying count.
- Published cybersecurity-market estimates disagree materially; they are directional only and are not used to derive this product's market.

## TAM, SAM and SOM definitions

- **Morocco TAM scenario:** all serviceable industrial sites and assessment partners under the assumptions above.
- **Initial SAM:** automotive/suppliers, phosphate-mining-chemicals, energy/water and port-logistics sites reachable through Casablanca–Tangier–Kenitra–Jorf/Safi channels.
- **Three-year SOM:** paying accounts the team and partners can onboard and support, constrained by safe field capacity rather than a percentage of TAM.

## Proposed Morocco pricing tests

| Package | Test price | Buyer | Included |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Readiness workshop | MAD 7,500–15,000 | SME/partner | scope, architecture and field plan |
| Controlled baseline | MAD 25,000–60,000/site | industrial owner | field collection, reconciliation and report |
| Professional | MAD 9,900/year | individual assessor | cases, passive analysis, standard packs |
| Team | MAD 29,000/year | integrator/MSSP | 3 seats, branded reports, exports |
| Industrial site | MAD 45,000/year | plant | governed workflows and site history |
| Enterprise/group | from MAD 120,000/year | multi-site owner | private packs, connectors, deployment controls |
| Field kit | MAD 2,500–7,500 | all | tested NIC/cables; TAP changes upper bound |

Prices are hypotheses designed to test procurement bands. Quote in MAD, invoice locally through a qualified entity/partner and avoid a mandatory USD cloud subscription.

## Unit economics template

Base partner-led Team sale:

| Item | Assumption |
|---|---:|
| First-year revenue | MAD 29,000 |
| Payment/hosting/signing/support COGS | MAD 4,500 |
| Gross profit | MAD 24,500 |
| Gross margin | 84% |
| Sales/enablement CAC | MAD 12,000 |
| First-year contribution | MAD 12,500 |
| Annual renewal revenue | MAD 29,000 |
| Renewal COGS | MAD 3,500 |
| Renewal contribution | MAD 25,500 |

Target guardrails: CAC payback under 12 months, software gross margin above 75%, annual logo retention above 85%, support below six hours/account/year after onboarding. These are management thresholds, not observed performance.

## Sensitivity

At MAD 29,000 Team ARR:

| Active customers | ARR | 80% gross profit | Meaning |
|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 25 | MAD 725k | MAD 580k | founder/early team validation |
| 75 | MAD 2.175m | MAD 1.740m | supports focused product/support team |
| 200 | MAD 5.8m | MAD 4.64m | credible Morocco/regional platform |
| 500 | MAD 14.5m | MAD 11.6m | requires multi-country channel |

If average support reaches 24 hours/customer/year at MAD 250/hour internal cost, COGS rises MAD 6,000 per customer before infrastructure or partner margin. Coverage discipline is therefore economic, not merely technical.

## Evidence to replace assumptions

- named count of sites per priority cluster;
- number of integrators/MSSPs with industrial clients;
- actual assessment frequency per partner;
- accepted fixed-scope quote;
- measured delivery hours and travel;
- conversion from baseline service to annual license;
- renewal and support usage.
