# Customer evidence map

## Primary segment: qualified industrial audit providers

| Evidence | Observable fact | Product consequence |
|---|---|---|
| DGSSI provider table | industrial audit is a distinct qualification domain | design for qualified audit workflow |
| DGSSI qualification criteria | methods and tools are evaluated | publish method/tool evidence, not marketing promises |
| periodic audit rule for applicable sensitive systems | recurring audit mechanism exists | potential repeat use, frequency must be verified per entity |
| three providers visibly qualified for industrial audits | finite named first research set | direct evidence-specification review is feasible |
| data/classification/cloud rules | sensitive-data handling is constrained | offline/minimized export is a design requirement, not assumed preference |

## Secondary segment: automation and industrial service providers

| Evidence | Observable fact | Product consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Renault, Suprajit, Givaudan and other job descriptions | personnel must work across PLC/SCADA/protocols | heterogeneous protocol catalog is justified |
| Jibal job description | technical documentation and CMMS follow-up are part of work | export/provenance may support existing maintenance records |
| ANP/ONEE tenders | telemanagement and automata require installation/maintenance | commissioning and maintenance are evidence-collection triggers |
| integrator job postings | skills span vendors and customer sites | portable repeatable tooling is a testable proposition |

## End-user segment

Large operators are evidenced as owners of relevant systems, but their buying preferences are not public. End-user StoryBrand language must therefore describe the work or rule, not claim motives.

## Evidence still absent

- exact audit workflow used by the three qualified industrial providers;
- current tools and dissatisfaction;
- mobile-device acceptance or MDM restrictions;
- number of field engagements per year;
- Bluetooth/Wi-Fi audit demand;
- accepted evidence schema;
- procurement price and license preference;
- willingness to contribute device fingerprints;
- required professional liability coverage;
- whether a product vendor must itself be qualified.

## Allowed research request

> “Please review whether this evidence schema and packet-safety specification can fit your qualified industrial-audit methodology. We are requesting corrections, not asking you to endorse a product.”

This request is factual and produces decision evidence without an open-ended customer interview.
