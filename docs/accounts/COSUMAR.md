# Cosumar — account decision brief

_Public-source review: 30 August 2026. Historical employee roles and technology claims are labelled as such._

## Decision

**Pursue a pre-campaign inventory-reconciliation pilot at one factory through Industrial Operations and IT/Digital.** The strongest pain hypothesis is campaign readiness across mixed-generation automation, not a group-wide security scan.

## Why now

- Cosumar is Morocco's sole sugar operator, with multiple subsidiaries, agricultural regions and industrial sites ([Cosumar](https://www.cosumar.co.ma/en/who-we-are/)).
- The group says it is modernizing industrial facilities and deploying ISO 50001 across sites ([Cosumar sustainability](https://www.cosumar.co.ma/en/sustainability/)).
- SIAM 2026 coverage described major 2024/25 beet and white-sugar production despite drought, reinforcing the operational value of campaign readiness ([Le360](https://fr.le360.ma/economie/cosumar-au-siam-2026-performance-et-innovation-au-service-de-la-souverainete-sucriere_36XB52HXUZDW3GUDT67WEXC6WQ/)).

## Entry route

1. Ask the Industrial and IT/Digital directors to nominate one factory and one pre-campaign shutdown scope.
2. Engage the incumbent automation integrator; import the maintenance list and use passive collection by default.
3. Measure unknown assets resolved, stale records corrected and readiness-review time.

## Stop/go questions

- Who currently leads automation/instrumentation at group and plant level?
- Which CMMS/inventory is authoritative?
- What Yokogawa/Rockwell versions and network boundaries exist at the selected plant?
- Are Android, USB Ethernet, radios and packet capture permitted in food-production areas?
