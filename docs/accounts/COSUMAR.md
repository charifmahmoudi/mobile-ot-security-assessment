# Cosumar

## Operating context

Cosumar is Morocco's sole sugar operator and groups five companies: Cosumar SA, SUTA, Sucrafor, Surac and Sunabel. Public company material describes activity across five regions; industry profiles describe nine industrial sites and a network of roughly 80,000 growers. The combination of seasonal processing, refining, utilities and distributed plants makes uptime and current asset records operationally important.

## Publicly visible organization

Cosumar publishes unusually useful management information for account planning:

| Role | Publicly named person | Why the role matters |
|---|---|---|
| CEO | Amine Louali | Executive sponsorship |
| IT & Digital Transformation Director | Amine Lahbichi | Security, architecture, data and integration gate |
| Industrial Director | Abdelmotalib El Abbadi | Plant operations and maintenance sponsor |
| Sales/marketing/supply chain | Anas Jamal Eddine | Business continuity and multi-site flow impact |

The likely pilot needs joint digital and industrial ownership. Neither side alone should authorize work on a production network.

## Technology signals

A public professional history tied to four Cosumar factories names Yokogawa CS3000/CENTUM VP DCS work. A Moroccan automation contractor publicly describes Cosumar sugar-line automation, PID control and SCADA supervision. These are useful site/vendor clues, but contract dates and exact current deployments must be revalidated.

Process-industry protocol priorities should therefore include passive Modbus/TCP and OPC UA clues, general Ethernet inventory and import of vendor/CMMS records. Yokogawa-specific identification should be designed as a signed, reviewable module rather than baked into the application.

## Account entry thesis

Pitch the harvest/production campaign preparation problem: before a seasonal run, maintenance teams need to know whether the networked automation inventory and documentation still match the plant. Atlas OT Scout could support an authorized walkdown, passive capture and discrepancy report during planned shutdown work.

A first pilot should target one utility or packaging area, not the core refining process. Measure the number of inventory discrepancies resolved and the time to prepare the maintenance record. If useful, expand through the Industrial Director's multi-site organization with IT/digital governance.

## Events and relationship surface

Cosumar is active in Morocco's agro-industrial ecosystem and communicates through its corporate and LinkedIn channels. The more direct relationship route is the automation/instrumentation partner base and AUSIM, where Moroccan IT leaders discuss cybersecurity maturity. Event attendance is an introduction mechanism, not proof of budget.

## What must be checked

- Current control vendors by site and whether legacy systems remain in production.
- Whether IT & Digital Transformation owns OT security or shares it with Industrial Operations.
- Approved integrators and maintenance framework contracts.
- Campaign/shutdown calendar suitable for a pilot.
- Existing CMMS/asset inventory and the cost of inaccurate records.
