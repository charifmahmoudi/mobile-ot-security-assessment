# SRM Béni Mellal–Khénifra account dossier

_Research baseline: **1 September 2026**. Qualification research only; no purchasing relationship or system access is implied._

## Account

| Priority | Status | Geography | Water scope |
|---|---|---|---|

## Verified public evidence

- **Regional programme:** A 2026 management interview describes a broad investment programme across five provinces.
- **Treatment projects:** Current procurement includes a wastewater-treatment plant at Tizi N'Isly.
- **Network works:** Current notices include Khouribga sanitation reinforcement and water-leak repair in Béni Mellal and Khénifra provinces.

## Current public signals

- **Treatment and collection growth:** New and rehabilitated wastewater facilities create clear commissioning and asset-baseline use cases.
- **Water-loss operations:** Leak repair and network performance are visible procurement themes.
- **Distributed estate:** Five provinces imply rural, urban and industrial interfaces with varied evidence maturity.

## Atlas entry wedge

Lead with one treatment-plant commissioning package or a water-loss district where the project owner needs a reconciled asset and evidence record before acceptance.

Keep the first engagement to one operator, one process area and one decision. Passive or imported evidence is the default; any packet-producing action requires exact written authorization.

## Buying group and qualification

**Roles to identify:** Regional and provincial water operations; wastewater treatment and sanitation; network performance/leak management; project engineering and acceptance; asset management/GIS; maintenance; IS/cybersecurity.

**Questions:** Which treatment or network project has a defined handover date? Who owns upstream-supply and downstream-treatment interfaces? What asset identifiers are used in work orders and GIS? Can the pilot use commissioning evidence before connecting to operations?

**Risks:** Do not infer an OCP relationship solely from geography. Tender mirrors require confirmation on the official portal. Treat each province as a separate qualification exercise.

Public sources do not prove installed vendors, protocols, topology, vulnerabilities, budget availability or permission to test.
