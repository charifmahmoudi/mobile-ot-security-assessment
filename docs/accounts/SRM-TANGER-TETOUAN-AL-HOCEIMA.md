# SRM Tanger–Tétouan–Al Hoceïma account brief

## Water-sector scope

The relevant scope is drinking-water networks, leak detection and repair, new water connections, flow/pressure instrumentation and liquid-sanitation networks across the northern regional perimeter.

The electricity function of the multiservice company is expressly outside this account brief.

## Current public signals

- Current 2026 procurement notices show active drinking-water network, leak-repair and sanitation programs.
- The broad geographic perimeter makes province and asset ownership a mandatory first question.
- Procurement notices may name an administrative contact; that does not establish technical ownership or pilot authority.

## Recommended first offer

Propose a commissioning-evidence pack for one flowmeter, pressure-management, leak-reduction or network-renewal project. Begin with contractor documents and an approved passive capture, not broad discovery.

## Buying group to validate

- water operations or provincial water director;
- wastewater operations where applicable;
- automation, instrumentation, SCADA or telemanagement owner;
- maintenance and asset-management lead;
- information-systems/cybersecurity representative;
- project management and public procurement;
- incumbent integrator or engineering contractor for the selected site.

## Pilot boundary

Use one named treatment, pumping, storage, pressure-management, district-metering or wastewater area. Start with an approved inventory and passive evidence. Do not scan the regional estate, cross into electricity networks or infer installed products from public tender language.

## Source basis

- Current public procurement notices from the regional company.
- Official or legacy regional utility pages describing the SRM water and sanitation mandate.

Public sources establish organizational and project relevance only. They do not prove technology versions, cyber posture, budget or buying authority.
