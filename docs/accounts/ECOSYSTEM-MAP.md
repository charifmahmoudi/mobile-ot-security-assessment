# Morocco water delivery ecosystem

_Research baseline: **1 September 2026**._

This map distinguishes **customers** from companies and institutions that finance, engineer, integrate or operate parts of Moroccan water infrastructure. Presence in an article or project does not make an organisation a buyer for Atlas OT Scout.

## Named ecosystem organisations

| Organisation | Publicly evidenced role | Account relevance | Classification |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Veolia** | Former Lydec shareholder and group behind delegated-service activities including Redal/Amendis contexts | Transition intelligence and possible operating/technical stakeholder | Legacy operator / industrial partner |
| **ADI / Nareva** | Reported engineering winner for SRM Oriental's priority water and sanitation programme | Project-design and delivery influence | Engineering / project partner |
| **JESA** | OCP identifies JESA among partners in its green-water ecosystem | Engineering, EPC and handover route around OCP projects | Engineering / EPC |
| **IFC** | Financing partner for OCP desalination, treated-water and pipeline programme | Governance, environmental and project-evidence requirements | Development-finance institution |
| **EBRD** | Financing partner for ONEE modernisation and OCP desalination facilities | Procurement, safeguards and acceptance context | Development-finance institution |
| **AFD / European Union** | Official SRM-FM tender notices identify co-financing for sectorisation and pressure-management projects | Procurement and project-governance context | Development partners |
| **CDG / CDG Capital** | Arranged OCP Green Water financing announced in 2025 | Financing and programme governance | Financial partner |
| **Local civil, hydraulic and electromechanical contractors** | Named in public awards and procurement results across SRMs | Delivery and as-built evidence; not necessarily system authority | Contractors |

## Engagement logic

| Situation | Lead account | Ecosystem route |
|---|---|---|
| Existing operating plant or network | Operator: ONEE, SRM or OCP Green Water | Maintenance contractor and incumbent integrator after operator approval |
| New project before handover | Project owner/operator | EPC, engineer and integrator as delivery participants |
| Development-financed programme | Operator/project owner | Procurement and safeguard requirements from the financing programme |
| Transition between operators | Current legal operator | Predecessor records and transition team as evidence sources |
| Technology demonstration | Operator lab/training owner | Approved integrator or engineering partner |

## Prohibited inference

Do not infer that an engineering firm, financier or named contractor:

- owns the production network;
- can authorize testing;
- uses a particular control-system vendor;
- has a commercial mandate covering the entire account;
- is willing to resell or sponsor Atlas.

These are qualification questions.
