# SRM Marrakech–Safi account dossier

_Research baseline: **1 September 2026**. Qualification research only; no purchasing relationship or system access is implied._

## Account

| Priority | Status | Geography | Water scope |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — major water-security and reuse account | Public regional multiservice company | Marrakech–Safi region | Regional drinking-water distribution, liquid sanitation and electricity services |

## Verified public evidence

- **Major drinking-water projects:** The company publicly described two programmes for Grand Marrakech and rural Safi totalling about MAD 4.7bn.
- **Sanitation renewal:** 2026 procurement includes sewer rehabilitation by lining in Safi and additional engineering studies.
- **Reuse interface:** OCP reports receiving treated wastewater from the Marrakech treatment plant for Benguerir/Gantour operations since June 2025.

## Current public signals

- **Urban/rural mix:** The portfolio spans metropolitan growth, rural supply and industrial reuse.
- **Cross-company interface:** The Marrakech wastewater-to-OCP transfer creates a concrete operator/industrial-customer handoff boundary.
- **Large project pipeline:** Reservoir, pumping, water-security and sanitation projects offer multiple bounded pilot candidates.

## Atlas entry wedge

Prioritise either a Grand Marrakech hydraulic project, a Safi rural-water package, or the treated-wastewater transfer interface. Frame the pilot around project acceptance and evidence continuity between the SRM, contractor and receiving operator.

Keep the first engagement to one operator, one process area and one decision. Passive or imported evidence is the default; any packet-producing action requires exact written authorization.

## Buying group and qualification

**Roles to identify:** Water investment and operations; Safi and Marrakech provincial directions; sanitation and reuse operations; project acceptance and engineering; asset management/GIS; OT/SCADA and information systems; OCP interface owner where relevant.

**Questions:** Who owns each side of the treated-water transfer boundary? Which project has an acceptance or inventory-reconciliation milestone? Are passive captures or commissioning records available? What data may leave the site, and what must remain on the appliance?

**Risks:** Do not imply that OCP and the SRM share one network or security authority. A project value does not equal software budget. Confirm exact ownership of treatment, transfer, pumping and distribution assets.

Public sources do not prove installed vendors, protocols, topology, vulnerabilities, budget availability or permission to test.
