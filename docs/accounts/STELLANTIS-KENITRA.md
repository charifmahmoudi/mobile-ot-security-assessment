# Stellantis Kenitra

## Why the timing matters

Stellantis inaugurated a major Kenitra expansion in July 2025. Reuters reported a €1.2 billion program intended to lift annual capacity to 535,000 vehicles, increase light electric-vehicle production from 20,000 to 70,000, add hybrid engines and three-wheelers, and raise local sourcing from 69% toward 75% by 2030. Commissioning and ramp-up create repeated moments when teams need to reconcile equipment, vendors and network connections.

## Publicly visible organization

| Layer | Public signal | Relevance |
|---|---|---|
| Regional operations | Samir Cherfan publicly represented Stellantis Middle East & Africa at the expansion | Regional executive sponsor |
| Site leadership | Mounir Kharbouche publicly identifies as VP/General Manager, Kenitra Automotive Industrial Complex | Site accountability |
| Project/equipment teams | Current public roles include Equipment Leader and industrial project leadership | Likely users/reviewers during installation and ramp-up |
| IT/cyber | Public profiles indicate regional IT leadership, but a current named Kenitra OT-security owner was not reliably established | Must be resolved before outreach |

This map does not prove an internal reporting chain. A likely approval path is equipment/project team → plant IT/automation and safety → site general management → regional/group cybersecurity and procurement.

## Technology signals

Public Stellantis Kenitra hiring confirms automation technician and equipment-lead demand. A Morocco industrial project profile references industrial automation and diagnostics. The sources do not responsibly establish a plant-wide vendor stack, so the product backlog should not label Kenitra as “Siemens” or “Rockwell” without a stronger source.

The more important engineering implication is change control: during commissioning, any active identification packet must be tied to an approved profile and maintenance window. Passive capture and inventory-import reconciliation should be the default.

## Account entry thesis

Offer a commissioning evidence pack for one new or modified cell: authorized connection record, passive capture, device/interface observations, vendor clues with confidence, and a delta against the handover inventory. Position it as documentation quality and review speed, not penetration testing.

An integrator or equipment supplier already contracted for the expansion may be a more realistic first route than unsolicited enterprise sales. The supplier can validate whether the workflow helps acceptance testing while Stellantis retains approval authority.

## Events and relationship surface

Kenitra site leadership publicly shares plant visits and industrial-innovation activity. The account is also embedded in Morocco's automotive supplier ecosystem and IFMIA training network. Industry Meeting Day and automotive supplier events are useful for identifying project and integrator organizations; they are not proof of purchase intent.

## What must be checked

- Current plant IT/OT security owner and global Stellantis policy owner.
- Expansion EPCs, line builders and approved automation integrators.
- Handover inventory format and recurring documentation gaps.
- Approved mobile/USB hardware and whether cameras/Bluetooth must be disabled.
- Supplier onboarding, NDA and procurement route.
