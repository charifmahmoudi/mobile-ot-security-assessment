# Morocco water-utility engagement playbook

## Positioning

Atlas OT Scout is a field evidence instrument for a bounded water or wastewater assessment. It helps an authorized team reconcile an inventory, preserve evidence provenance and expose what remains unknown. It is not a general scanner, exploitation platform or continuous monitoring replacement.

## Use cases to lead with

- treatment-plant or pumping-station inventory reconciliation;
- reservoir and remote-station telemanagement baseline;
- district-metering or flowmeter commissioning evidence;
- leak-reduction project handover;
- wastewater-plant or collection-network acceptance;
- contractor-to-operator documentation reconciliation;
- pre-audit evidence preparation for one process area.

## Discovery questions

- Who legally operates this exact asset?
- Is the objective commissioning, maintenance, audit preparation or incident-free inventory cleanup?
- Which inventory is considered authoritative?
- Can the team provide an approved capture rather than connecting a device?
- Which protocols and vendors are documented, rather than guessed?
- Who reviews operations, automation, security and evidence retention?
- What conditions stop the assessment immediately?

## Pilot stages

1. **Scope:** name site, process area, owner, reviewers and prohibited actions.
2. **Prepare:** import the approved inventory and topology.
3. **Collect:** passive evidence first; use receive-only capture only where authorized.
4. **Review:** accept, reject, merge or leave each observation unresolved.
5. **Close one gap:** optional one-target identity request with a signed, expiring grant.
6. **Handoff:** deliver evidence-linked changes, limitations and blockers.

## Commercial proof

The first commercial proof is not a claim that the platform discovers every device. It is a jointly reviewed before/after inventory and an evidence package that an operator and contractor can defend.

## Safety language

Never say “scan the plant,” “find vulnerabilities,” or “prove security.” Say “reconcile one authorized inventory,” “observe an approved evidence source,” and “document remaining uncertainty.”
