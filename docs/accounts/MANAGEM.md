# Managem Group

## Operating context

Managem reports 13 mining operations, 20 industrial units, 5,000 employees and activity across seven African countries. Its Morocco portfolio includes established and developing sites; different mines may use different operating companies, engineering teams and control stacks. A group introduction does not authorize a site assessment.

The Tizert copper project is the clearest current opening. In November 2024, Epiroc announced that Managem selected it to provide digital tools intended to improve safety and productivity at Tizert. Public project work also references Siemens PCS 7 and WinCC at Tizert. Together these sources show active digitalization and a concrete control-system signal, without establishing the entire plant architecture.

## Public activity and events

Managem participated in the Marrakech Mining Convention, where digitalization, safety and sustainable development were central themes. Its own site publishes procurement access. Mining events can identify engineering partners, but the stronger signal is the disclosed Tizert program: a live project with safety and productivity objectives.

## Account entry thesis

Position Atlas OT Scout as a handover and maintenance evidence tool for Tizert or a Reminex-managed project. It could reconcile vendor/EPC documentation against passive observations and produce a signed exception list for Managem review. It should not compete with Epiroc's mining-operations platform; the integration question is whether portable field evidence can feed the project record.

Mining adds constraints the prototype must demonstrate: offline operation, rugged USB hardware, battery management, dust/heat tolerance of the selected device, strict radio controls and safe working procedures. A consumer phone without an approved case and power plan is not a finished field product.

## What must be checked

- Current CISO/OT-security owner and relationship with COO Morocco.
- Reminex's responsibility for automation, commissioning and asset documentation.
- Tizert EPC, control vendor, network architecture and Epiroc integration boundary.
- Whether radios must be physically disabled underground or in process areas.
- Group procurement versus operating-subsidiary procurement.
