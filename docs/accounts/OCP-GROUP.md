# OCP Group

## Why this account is structurally relevant

OCP operates phosphate mines and chemical-production platforms across Morocco, including Khouribga, Benguerir, Youssoufia, Safi and the Jorf Lasfar complex. OCP describes Jorf Lasfar as the world's largest phosphate-fertilizer production platform. This is the kind of distributed, brownfield process environment where asset identity, temporary contractor access and mixed generations of control equipment can matter.

OCP is not an undifferentiated parent company. The route to a field tool is likely to pass through a site or operating subsidiary, then group security and procurement. OCP Maintenance Solutions (OCP-MS) is particularly relevant: in January 2026 it announced cooperation with the International Centre for Industrial Transformation to deliver industrial-maturity and transformation assessments in Morocco and Africa. That makes OCP-MS both a potential internal user and a possible channel for an assessment instrument.

## Technology and activity signals

- OCP's strategy page says digital, data and AI are used to streamline operations.
- Its 2022 “Usine du Futur” challenge invited startups to co-develop Industry 4.0 solutions, indicating an established mechanism for testing emerging industrial technology.
- A public Benguerir operations profile mentions Honeywell DCS and SCADA. This is a site-specific signal, not proof of a group-wide standard.
- OCP's industrial footprint includes long-distance material movement and large continuous-process sites. A phone connected to an ordinary USB-Ethernet adapter cannot observe an entire switched segment; the product must support an authorized mirror/TAP or a supplied capture file.

## Events and partner ecosystem

OCP Solutions participated in the MoroccoAI 2024 conference, and OCP publicly runs innovation/open-challenge programs. OCP-MS's 2026 INCIT cooperation is more commercially relevant than a generic trade show: it points to an organization already packaging industrial assessments. OCP's joint ventures at Jorf Lasfar also mean a project may have operating-company and partner approvals beyond OCP Group alone.

## Account entry thesis

Lead with a controlled assessment workflow, not broad vulnerability scanning: “give an authorized OCP-MS or site engineer a repeatable way to record what is connected during a maintenance walkdown, attach packet evidence, and export it for OT-security review.” Start at one non-production training environment or a scheduled maintenance window. The first success metric is reconciliation quality and time, not number of vulnerabilities found.

The strongest introduction paths are:

1. OCP OT cybersecurity, with OCP-MS as operating partner.
2. An OCP-approved industrial systems auditor already qualified under the DGSSI scheme.
3. A site automation/reliability team at Jorf Lasfar or Benguerir with group security present.

## What must be checked before approaching

- Whether OCP-MS already uses a commercial discovery platform.
- Which entity owns the device inventory at the proposed site.
- Whether Android, Bluetooth and USB peripherals are permitted inside the site.
- Approved passive collection point and data classification/export rules.
- Whether the pilot must be bought through an existing framework supplier.
