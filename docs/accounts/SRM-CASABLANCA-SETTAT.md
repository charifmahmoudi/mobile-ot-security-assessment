# SRM Casablanca–Settat account brief

## Water-sector scope

The relevant scope is drinking-water distribution, water-quality operations, reservoirs, pumping, network performance and liquid sanitation across the regional company's managed perimeter.

The electricity function of the multiservice company is expressly outside this account brief.

## Current public signals

- The regional company began operating in October 2024 under Morocco's regional multiservice-company framework.
- Its official service pages identify drinking-water distribution and liquid sanitation, including water-quality monitoring and wastewater-treatment assets.
- Public service and procurement routes are preferable to guessed individual contacts.

## Recommended first offer

Propose a single-zone inventory reconciliation around a reservoir, pumping station, pressure zone or wastewater-treatment plant. The output should support maintenance planning and acceptance of an integrator handover.

## Buying group to validate

- water operations or provincial water director;
- wastewater operations where applicable;
- automation, instrumentation, SCADA or telemanagement owner;
- maintenance and asset-management lead;
- information-systems/cybersecurity representative;
- project management and public procurement;
- incumbent integrator or engineering contractor for the selected site.

## Pilot boundary

Use one named treatment, pumping, storage, pressure-management, district-metering or wastewater area. Start with an approved inventory and passive evidence. Do not scan the regional estate, cross into electricity networks or infer installed products from public tender language.

## Source basis

- Official SRM-CS service and organization pages.
- Public procurement portal for current water and sanitation projects.

Public sources establish organizational and project relevance only. They do not prove technology versions, cyber posture, budget or buying authority.
