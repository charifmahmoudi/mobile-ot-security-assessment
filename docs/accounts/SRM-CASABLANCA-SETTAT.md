# SRM Casablanca–Settat account dossier

_Research baseline: **1 September 2026**. Qualification research only; no purchasing relationship or system access is implied._

## Account

| Priority | Status | Geography | Water scope |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — largest regional distribution account | Public regional multiservice company; operational since 1 October 2024 | Casablanca–Settat region | Drinking-water distribution, liquid sanitation, electricity distribution and public-lighting responsibilities inherited from multiple operators |

## Verified public evidence

- **Operator transition:** The official SRM-CS site says the company consolidated Lydec, RADEEJ, RADEEC and regional ONEE distribution activities.
- **Investment scale:** Management publicly described a programme of about MAD 61bn serving more than seven million people.
- **Project portfolio:** The official procurement programme includes reservoirs, pumping and booster equipment, water-network sectorisation, renewal, water-quality analysis and sanitation works.

## Current public signals

- **High transition complexity:** Multiple predecessor organisations create a need to reconcile data models, asset registers and operating practices.
- **Reservoir and pumping expansion:** Published plans include new storage and hydraulic complexes, making commissioning and handover a practical entry point.
- **Sectorisation and rehabilitation:** Water-efficiency and network-renewal programmes create repeatable evidence-baseline use cases.

## Atlas entry wedge

Propose one provincial or municipal water/sanitation package tied to a named reservoir, pumping complex, sectorisation project or wastewater area. The deliverable should reconcile predecessor records, current field evidence and project-handover documentation.

Keep the first engagement to one operator, one process area and one decision. Passive or imported evidence is the default; any packet-producing action requires exact written authorization.

## Buying group and qualification

**Roles to identify:** Regional water operations; provincial water directorate; hydraulic works and project acceptance; sanitation operations; asset management and GIS; information systems/cybersecurity; procurement and incumbent integrators.

**Questions:** Which predecessor owned the target assets, and who owns the current record? Is the target a distribution zone, reservoir, pumping station, treatment interface or sewer facility? Which project-acceptance documents and contractor inventories exist? Does the area have an approved passive observation point?

**Risks:** Do not treat all seven million residents or the full regional estate as the pilot scope. Confirm whether a site is under SRM-CS, ONEE production or another project owner. Historical Lydec information is transition context, not automatically current technical truth.

Public sources do not prove installed vendors, protocols, topology, vulnerabilities, budget availability or permission to test.
