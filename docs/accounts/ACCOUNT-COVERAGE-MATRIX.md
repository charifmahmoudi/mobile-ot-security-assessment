## Coverage model

| Layer | Included organisations | Commercial interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| National production and conveyance | ONEE Water Branch | Primary route for treatment, pumping, desalination, transmission and telemanagement evidence |
| Regional distribution and sanitation | All 12 SRMs | Primary route for distribution, reservoirs, pumping, sewerage, treatment, network performance and regional asset data |
| Industrial non-conventional water | OCP Green Water | Primary route for desalination, reuse, transfer corridors and industrial project handover |
| Delegated/predecessor operators | Lydec, Redal, Amendis | Transition intelligence; current buyer status must be confirmed per perimeter |
| Delivery ecosystem | Engineering, EPC, systems integration and development-finance partners | Channel, influence or delivery role; not automatically a customer |

## Evidence standard

Each current-account dossier contains:

1. legal/operational status and geographic scope;
2. verified company facts;
3. dated projects, procurement signals and press mentions;
4. a bounded Atlas entry offer;
5. explicitly labelled hypotheses;
6. functional buying and review roles;
7. qualification questions and disqualifiers;
8. source URLs and evidence grades.

Source IDs resolve in [the web source register](research/WEB-SOURCE-REGISTER.csv). A source supports only the claim recorded in its row.

## Portfolio gaps that remain

Comprehensive public-source coverage does **not** establish:

- installed OT vendors, protocols, firmware or network topology;
- a confirmed project sponsor or available budget;
- authorization to access any site or system;
- whether a published procurement was awarded, cancelled or changed after the research date;
- the current contractual boundary where a legacy delegated operator remains visible.

Those points require direct qualification, official procurement verification and written authorization.
