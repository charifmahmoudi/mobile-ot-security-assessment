## Verified public evidence

- **Enterprise GIS:** A 2026 procurement notice seeks an integrated institutional GIS and web-based business-GIS platform.
- **Sanitation renewal:** A current project covers extension and rehabilitation of the Ouarzazate liquid-sanitation network.
- **Water operations:** Current notices cover maintenance and new connections in Boumalne Dades and Tamgroute.

## Current public signals

- **Direct data-modernisation fit:** The GIS programme creates a clear need for field provenance, conflict handling and controlled ingestion.
- **Remote operating context:** Large distances and provincial centres favour offline, portable evidence collection.
- **Mixed renewal work:** Water maintenance and sanitation rehabilitation generate frequent inventory deltas.

## Atlas entry wedge

Offer Atlas as a field-evidence layer alongside the institutional GIS: one province, one work package, one controlled import/export mapping and a documented conflict-resolution review.

Keep the first engagement to one operator, one process area and one decision. Passive or imported evidence is the default; any packet-producing action requires exact written authorization.

## Buying group and qualification

**Roles to identify:** GIS programme owner; water and sanitation operations; provincial directorates; project and maintenance teams; data governance/IS; automation or telemanagement where present; procurement.

**Questions:** What is the target GIS schema and unique asset identifier? Which data is being migrated, and which must be field-verified? Can Atlas export only reviewed evidence deltas? Which provincial project can be isolated for a proof of workflow?

**Risks:** Do not position Atlas as a replacement for the enterprise GIS. Public tenders do not prove the current GIS vendor or implementation status. Field travel, device custody and offline-retention controls must be planned.

Public sources do not prove installed vendors, protocols, topology, vulnerabilities, budget availability or permission to test.
