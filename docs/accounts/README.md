# Morocco water-sector target accounts

This commercial research pack is intentionally limited to **drinking-water and wastewater operations in Morocco**. It excludes automotive, aerospace, mining, ports, phosphate, food-processing and electricity-only opportunities.

The initial account set covers the national water operator and regional multiservice companies only where the proposed work is explicitly scoped to water production, treatment, pumping, storage, distribution, wastewater collection or treatment.

## Account decisions

| Account | Water-sector scope | Recommended first offer | Best evidenced route |
|---|---|---|---|
| [ONEE — Water Branch](ONEE.md) | National drinking-water production, transmission, rural supply and sanitation programs | One controlled treatment or pumping-station evidence baseline | Water operations, automation/telemanagement and the approved integrator |
| [SRM Casablanca–Settat](SRM-CASABLANCA-SETTAT.md) | Drinking-water distribution and liquid sanitation | Network-zone or wastewater-plant inventory reconciliation | Water operations, provincial directorate and public procurement |
| [SRM Rabat–Salé–Kénitra](SRM-RABAT-SALE-KENITRA.md) | Regional drinking-water distribution and liquid sanitation outside the delegated Redal perimeter | Pumping/reservoir handover evidence pack | Water operations, asset management and information systems |
| [SRM Marrakech–Safi](SRM-MARRAKECH-SAFI.md) | Regional drinking-water and sanitation services | Treatment, pumping or district-metering pilot | General management, water operations and procurement |
| [SRM Fès–Meknès](SRM-FES-MEKNES.md) | Regional drinking-water and sanitation infrastructure | Reservoir, pipe-renewal or wastewater-project baseline | General management, provincial water operations and project teams |
| [SRM Tanger–Tétouan–Al Hoceïma](SRM-TANGER-TETOUAN-AL-HOCEIMA.md) | Drinking-water networks, leak reduction and liquid sanitation | Flowmeter/pressure-zone commissioning evidence | Water operations, maintenance and procurement |

## Research and outreach rules

- A multiservice company is in scope only for its **water and liquid-sanitation** responsibilities.
- Prefer official organization pages, procurement notices and named public professional profiles.
- Do not infer email addresses, budgets, reporting lines, installed products or vulnerabilities.
- Recheck titles and procurement routes on the day of outreach.
- Request a lab topology, redacted inventory or authorized one-area pilot before discussing production assessment.
- Active collection requires written authorization, an exact target and a documented stop condition.

Research baseline: **31 August 2026**.
