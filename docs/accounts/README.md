# Morocco account intelligence

This section treats potential customers as organizations rather than logos. It maps the operating environment, publicly visible decision roles, current projects, technology signals, partner ecosystem and realistic route into each account.

## How to read these dossiers

Each dossier separates three things:

- **Published fact** — supported by a linked company, government, procurement, event, partner or public professional source.
- **Commercial interpretation** — what that fact may mean for Atlas OT Scout. It is a hypothesis for account planning, not a statement by the company.
- **Open question** — information that cannot responsibly be established from public material and must be checked during a pilot or procurement conversation.

Names are included only when a person publicly identifies their professional role or appears on an official governance page. No private contact information or inferred email address is included. Titles and employment can change; re-check before outreach.

## What the account work changes

The early proposition should not be “a pentest phone.” The common opening across these accounts is a controlled field instrument that helps an authorized engineer collect an inventory snapshot and a traceable packet sample without carrying a laptop/cart of adapters or installing a permanent sensor. Enterprise accounts are unlikely to approve an unknown active scanner first. A credible entry is a narrowly bounded assessment run by their own team or an already trusted qualified auditor/integrator.

The product should therefore make authorization, interface selection, passive capture, capture provenance, reviewer approval and export first-class workflows. Protocol coverage comes second and exploit capability is deliberately excluded.

## Coverage and limitations

This is an account-research baseline dated 30 August 2026. It is not a claim that any named organization wants to buy the product. Public pages reveal only part of an organization; the internal reporting line, budget owner, security architecture, approved hardware and supplier framework remain unknown until an authorized conversation or procurement document establishes them.
