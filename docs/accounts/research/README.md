## Evidence grades

| Grade | Meaning | Permitted use |
|---|---|---|
| A | Government, operator, corporate or development-finance primary source | Company status, mandate, official figures and announced projects |
| B | Established press reporting attributable official/company statements | Corroborated project, board and investment context |
| C | Public-tender mirror linking or referring to the official procurement portal | Current procurement signal; verify before action |
| D | Contextual lead or secondary analysis | Research question only |

A source proves only the claim recorded in its row. A tender does not prove award, implementation, installed technology or vulnerability. A public role or article mention does not prove buying authority.
