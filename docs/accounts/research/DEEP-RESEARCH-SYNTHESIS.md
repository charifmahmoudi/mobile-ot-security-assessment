# Deep-research synthesis

_This is a coverage assessment, not a sales forecast. Public sources reviewed through 30 August 2026._

## What the expanded evidence changes

The strongest defensible concept is **portable, controlled evidence collection for project handover, maintenance reconciliation and formal industrial assessments**. The research does not support positioning the phone as an autonomous penetration-testing platform inside production networks.

Three repeated buying conditions are visible:

1. **A scheduled operational moment:** new line, greenfield facility, shutdown, campaign or training exercise.
2. **An existing authority:** maintenance/engineering plus cybersecurity or industrial IT.
3. **An existing record:** design list, CMMS, PMIS, SCADA project file or maturity-assessment workbook that receives the result.

Without all three, the account is not pilot-ready.

## Account-specific conclusions

### OCP

- **New finding:** the INCIT agreement names four formal readiness indexes and an assessor-training centre. OCP-MS is the clearest route to distribution beyond one OCP site.
- **Constraint:** OCP's internal ecosystem may be a partner or a capable substitute. Discovery must start with a workflow gap, not features.
- **Next target:** OCP-MS deliverables, approved auditor/integrator frameworks and one site's asset-record owner.

### Renault Morocco

- **New finding:** the CIO narrative joins operational data, digital twins, remote AR maintenance and audited cyber defence. Inventory quality is an input to an existing digital model.
- **Constraint:** protocol names come from job requirements, not a complete Tangier or SOMACA inventory.
- **Next target:** line-builder contracts, plant cyber approval and the acceptance-data format.

### Stellantis Kénitra

- **New finding:** a 2026 manufacturing-edge security role describes Plant Cybersecurity Leaders and security-by-design for Edge, IoT and AI. It does not identify Kénitra's owner, but reveals the governance language a pilot must satisfy.
- **Constraint:** approved line builders and the current plant cyber lead remain unknown.
- **Next target:** equipment-package awards, supplier exhibitors and current Kénitra IT/cyber recruitment.

### Tanger Med

- **New finding:** the digital-twin PoC names TMPA Maintenance, TME Asset Management, the Digitalization Team and PHD Soft. This is a more precise buying group than “Tanger Med IT.”
- **Constraint:** TMPA, TME, PCS, Marsa Maroc and terminal operators control different systems.
- **Next target:** owner and change-control process for one bridge, utility or maintenance network.

### ONEE

- **New finding:** the EIB transmission program and Siemens/Atos smart-grid project show simultaneous infrastructure expansion and incumbent central platforms. Portable collection belongs at EPC handover or distributed maintenance, not the control-centre core.
- **Constraint:** public procurement and critical-infrastructure approvals may dominate the sales cycle.
- **Next target:** technical dossiers, EPC awards and the exact electricity- versus water-branch authority.

### Managem

- **New finding:** Tizert is operating, not only planned: Managem reports 2025 output. The governance page names Naoual Zine as Reminex General Manager and Youssef El Hajjam over procurement/supply chain.
- **Constraint:** the Epiroc scope, PCS 7 profile clue and other EPC packages must not be conflated.
- **Next target:** Tizert package boundaries, commissioning records and current CISO/security approval.

### Safran Morocco

- **New finding:** three facility timelines create separate qualification opportunities, while procurement and IT/security recruitment show organization-building in progress.
- **Constraint:** no reliable Moroccan PLC/DCS vendor map was found. Aerospace IP and configuration control will be stricter than generic OT practice.
- **Next target:** named project directors, EPCs/line builders and the required configuration-management output.

### Cosumar

- **New finding:** IT & Digital leader Amine Lahbichi is publicly connected to the Digital Morocco 2030 launch; the governance page provides a direct Industrial + IT/Digital executive pairing.
- **Constraint:** the best vendor evidence is historical. Current automation leadership and versions remain unresolved.
- **Next target:** 2025 annual-report modernization detail, framework integrators and one factory's pre-campaign plan.

## What materially strengthens this next

- Original tender specifications and award notices, not tender aggregators.
- Lists naming plant engineering, maintenance, cyber and procurement participants.
- Archived job descriptions with entity, location and revision date.
- EPC, line-builder and system-integrator award announcements.
- Annual-report passages linking capital projects to maintenance, digital or risk priorities.
- A redacted inventory template or commissioning deliverable from an authorized partner.

More employee names without these connections would increase volume, not decision quality.
