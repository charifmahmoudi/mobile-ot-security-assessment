# Morocco water-sector deep-research synthesis

_Research baseline: **1 September 2026**._

## Decision

The account universe is no longer a six-company shortlist. It covers the complete current Morocco water operating structure relevant to P0-WATER:

- ONEE Water Branch for national production, treatment, conveyance and sanitation programmes;
- all 12 SRMs for regional distribution, sanitation and local infrastructure;
- OCP Green Water for industrial desalination, wastewater reuse and long-distance transfer.

Legacy delegated operators are retained as transition boundaries, not automatically classified as active customers.

## Evidence-backed demand clusters

### 1. Regional consolidation and asset-data migration

SRM-CS publicly consolidates multiple predecessor operators. SRM Oriental has procured field recognition and GIS entry. SRM Drâa–Tafilalet is procuring an integrated institutional GIS. SRM-RSK and SRM-TTA still require explicit Redal/Amendis boundary confirmation.

**Atlas implication:** preserve source records, compare them with passive evidence and require reviewed merge decisions.

### 2. Telemanagement, pressure and network performance

SRM-FM's official procurement includes drinking-water telemanagement, pressure reducers, sectorisation, leak detection and operating-equipment maintenance. ONEE continues major pumping, treatment and transfer projects.

**Atlas implication:** tie exact asset identity and provenance to one project-acceptance or maintenance decision; do not infer leakage or risk from protocol presence.

### 3. Treatment, desalination and wastewater reuse

OCP Green Water is expanding desalination and treated-water corridors. Dakhla procures modular desalination/demineralisation operations. Béni Mellal–Khénifra has treatment-plant projects. Marrakech–Safi interfaces with OCP's treated-water supply.

**Atlas implication:** start at a treatment skid, pumping node or transfer boundary where owner, operator and evidence custodian can be named.

### 4. Large water-security and renewal programmes

Public reporting identifies major investment programmes across SRMs, including Casablanca–Settat, Souss–Massa, Marrakech–Safi, Rabat–Salé–Kénitra, Guelmim–Oued Noun, Laâyoune–Sakia El Hamra and Dakhla–Oued Ed-Dahab.

**Atlas implication:** use the investment figure only to establish strategic relevance. Qualify a specific project and decision before discussing a pilot.

### 5. Rural and geographically distributed operations

Souss–Massa, Oriental, Béni Mellal–Khénifra, Drâa–Tafilalet, Guelmim–Oued Noun and the southern SRMs cover dispersed sites and provincial centres.

**Atlas implication:** offline operation, device custody, bounded evidence and explicit visibility limitations are core requirements.

## Highest-confidence commercial wedges

1. SRM-FM telemanagement or sectorisation acceptance.
2. SRM Oriental GIS field-verification support.
3. SRM Drâa–Tafilalet GIS migration support.
4. ONEE treatment/pumping project handover.
5. OCP Green Water commissioning evidence.
6. SRM-CS predecessor-inventory reconciliation.
7. Marrakech–Safi reuse-transfer interface.
8. Dakhla modular-treatment or renewal-lot baseline.

## Unknowns requiring primary confirmation

- current operator and contractual perimeter for every site;
- project sponsor, decision date and available procurement route;
- authoritative inventory, GIS and work-order owner;
- incumbent EPC, integrator and maintenance contractor;
- installed equipment, protocol and network architecture;
- data classification, retention and export restrictions;
- safe lab, FAT/SAT or isolated commissioning environment;
- written authorization for any packet-producing operation.
