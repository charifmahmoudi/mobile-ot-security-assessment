# Morocco water-sector deep-research synthesis

## Decision

The business-development scope is now aligned with P0-WATER. The former cross-industry account set has been removed. The active portfolio contains ONEE's Water Branch and regional multiservice companies, restricted to drinking water and liquid sanitation.

## Strongest demand signals

1. **Asset and document reconciliation during regional utility consolidation.** SRMs inherit heterogeneous inventories, operating practices and contractor records.
2. **Reservoir, pipe-renewal and leak-reduction projects.** Current procurement notices create discrete handover points where evidence provenance matters.
3. **Pumping, treatment and telemanagement operations.** Distributed RTUs, PLCs, HMIs and engineering workstations fit a site-centered mobile workflow.
4. **Wastewater collection and treatment.** Operators need bounded evidence without interrupting process control.
5. **Contractor-to-operator acceptance.** A jointly reviewed evidence package is more defensible than an unreviewed discovery export.

## Product implication

The default demo and commercial artifact should use a water-treatment or pumping topology. Account examples, screenshots and talking points should refer to process areas such as intake, treatment, clear-water pumping, reservoir, pressure zone, district meter, wastewater lift station and treatment train.

## Evidence gaps requiring primary confirmation

- current technical owners by region and province;
- exact SRM/legacy/delegated-operator boundaries;
- approved inventory systems and export formats;
- incumbent integrators and maintenance contractors;
- installed vendors, protocol versions and network segmentation;
- data retention, cybersecurity and procurement requirements;
- whether a representative lab or training environment is available.

## Research standard

Use official organization pages and procurement notices first. Use public professional profiles only to identify a role hypothesis. Never convert a public project signal into a claim about vulnerability, installed product or permission to test.
