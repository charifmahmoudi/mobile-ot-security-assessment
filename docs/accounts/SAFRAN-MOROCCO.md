# Safran Morocco — account decision brief

_Public-source review: 30 August 2026. Safran's Moroccan businesses are separate entities; titles and new-project teams are changing rapidly._

## Decision

**Prioritize a commissioning-evidence standard for the new MidParc facilities, delivered through an approved line builder.** Aerospace quality, IP and cyber controls make a consumer-phone-style assessment unacceptable; Safran-controlled hardware and keys are prerequisites.

## Why now

- Safran reports more than 4,800 employees across ten Moroccan sites ([Safran](https://www.safran-group.com/countries/morocco)).
- A €120 million engine-MRO site is planned for 2027 and a €200 million LEAP-1A assembly line for 2028; Reuters says the latter will be Safran's only engine-assembly line outside France ([Reuters, 13 Oct 2025](https://www.reuters.com/business/aerospace-defense/safran-open-new-airbus-engine-assembly-line-morocco-2025-10-13/)).
- A €280 million landing-gear plant is planned for 2029 ([Reuters, 13 Feb 2026](https://www.reuters.com/business/aerospace-defense/safran-open-landing-gear-plant-morocco-2026-02-13/)).

## Entry route

1. Identify the EPC/line builder and Safran project-quality owner for one new facility.
2. Define a Safran-controlled handover pack: design-list import, passive observation, physical evidence and reviewer sign-off.
3. Run on a lab/utility cell first; Safran controls device image, keys and export.

## Stop/go questions

- Who leads industrial IT/cybersecurity for each new entity?
- Which EPCs and automation suppliers hold the packages?
- Which devices, radios, cameras and removable media are prohibited?
- Which configuration/quality system must receive the evidence?
