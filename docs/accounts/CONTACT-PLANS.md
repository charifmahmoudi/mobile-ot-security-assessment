# Water-sector contact plans

_Research baseline: **1 September 2026**._

These plans cover the 14 current Morocco P0-WATER accounts in the [coverage matrix](ACCOUNT-COVERAGE-MATRIX.md). They use public organisational and procurement routes; they do not publish guessed email addresses or treat tender contacts as general sales leads.

## Contact sequence

1. **Confirm operator and asset ownership.** Name the region, commune, facility, process area, producer/distributor boundary and any delegated operator.
2. **Anchor the request in a public signal.** Refer to a current project, GIS programme, telemanagement package, treatment asset, sectorisation programme or handover—not a generic cyber pitch.
3. **Ask for the functional owner.** Request redirection to water operations, sanitation, automation/telemanagement, asset management/GIS, project acceptance or OT security.
4. **Offer a document or lab review first.** Use a redacted inventory, sample evidence package, FAT/SAT record or representative capture.
5. **Define one decision.** Examples: accept a contractor handover, reconcile an inventory, verify one controller identity, or document report-readiness blockers.
6. **Agree data governance and authorization.** Only then discuss a field pilot.

## Opening message

> We are validating a passive-first field workflow that preserves evidence while reconciling an authorized water or wastewater asset inventory. Your public programme for **[specific project]** appears to create a project-acceptance or handover question. We are seeking the functional owner of one bounded area who can review a redacted evidence package or an isolated demonstration. We are not requesting production access or proposing a broad network scan.

## Required discovery answers

- current legal operator and asset owner;
- exact site/process boundary;
- project phase and decision date;
- authoritative inventory/GIS owner;
- approved passive evidence source;
- incumbent EPC, integrator and maintenance contractor;
- technical, operations, security and data reviewers;
- retention/export restrictions;
- written scope and stop conditions for any field activity.

## Disallowed practices

Do not scrape or infer personal email addresses, contact employees outside a public professional context, reuse tender contacts for unrelated marketing, imply that a public project reveals a weakness, or ask an executive to authorize production testing.
