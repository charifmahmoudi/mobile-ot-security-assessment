# Tanger Med — account decision brief

_Public-source review: 30 August 2026. Tanger Med Group, TMPA, terminals and industrial-zone tenants are separate authorization domains._

## Decision

**Pursue Tanger Med Port Authority (TMPA) through operational information systems, with a narrowly scoped port-utility or handover pilot.** Do not market a generic network scan across the port ecosystem.

## Why now

- Tanger Med publicly describes digitization as central to port operations and “cyber resiliency” across its value chain ([Tanger Med](https://www.tangermed.ma/en/digitization/)).
- Its Port Community System reports 88 interconnected systems and 40,696 messages per day ([Tanger Med PCS](https://www.tangermedpcs.com/en/home)).
- The port is deploying digital twins/predictive maintenance and co-developing a next-generation PMIS with Wärtsilä ([digital twin](https://www.tangermed.ma/en/digital-twins-and-predictive-maintenance-a-major-advancement-for-tanger-med/), [PMIS](https://www.tangermedport.com/en/wartsila-and-tanger-med-team-up-to-co-develop-next-generation-port-management-information-system/)).

## Entry route

1. Approach TMPA operational IS via TOC Africa/chainPORT/1337 ecosystem, not individual terminal networks.
2. Pilot on one authorized utility, lab or commissioning zone; passive capture only by default.
3. Export signed exceptions into the existing operational information/maintenance workflow.

## Stop/go questions

- Which legal entity owns the target network and data?
- Where is the boundary between TMPA, PCS and terminal-operator security?
- Which permanent OT/network tools already exist?
- Are Android, USB Ethernet and packet capture approved in the selected zone?
