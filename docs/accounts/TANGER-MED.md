# Tanger Med Group account map

_Research baseline: 30 August 2026. Tanger Med includes several legal entities and independent terminal operators; every network scope must name its owner._

## Organization and operating boundaries

Tanger Med Group operates ports, logistics and industrial platforms. Its public site reports 209 million tons handled, 12.42 million TEU across the wider group, more than 3,000 hectares of economic activity zones, and more than 1,500 tenant companies. Through Marsa Maroc, the group also has terminal activity beyond Tangier.

The account must be split before any technical discussion:

| Entity/domain | Published responsibility | Authorization consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Tanger Med Special Agency / Group | Strategy, investment and group management | Group policy and sponsorship |
| Tanger Med Port Authority (TMPA) | Port infrastructure, port community coordination and service reliability | Owns common port authority scope, not every terminal network |
| Tanger Med Port Community System (PCS) | Multi-service digital platform connecting public/private actors | Enterprise/platform security, not necessarily crane/terminal OT |
| Tanger Med Engineering | Engineering and asset-management activity | Infrastructure/maintenance collection route |
| Tanger Med Zones | Industrial-zone development and tenant ecosystem | A tenant must authorize its own factory/network |
| Terminal operators | Operate container and other terminals | Separate OT asset owners and buying centers |

### Reconstructed approval path

For a TMPA infrastructure assessment, the likely path is maintenance/asset management → Technology & IT Security → cybersecurity/digital governance → TMPA management/procurement. For a terminal or tenant factory, replace TMPA ownership with the operator's own operations and security chain while Tanger Med controls site access and shared infrastructure.

## What users and partners say

The Tanger Med PCS site reports 88 interconnected systems and tens of thousands of electronic messages per day. Published users say digitization has reduced trips and processing time, improved traceability and provided real-time port visibility. An APM Terminals project director describes integration between shipping-line, terminal-operator and Tanger Med systems to improve dispatch and turnaround.

This commentary reveals the dominant customer value language: reliable information exchange, visibility, turnaround time and multi-party integration. Atlas OT Scout should speak to traceable field evidence and integration—not generic vulnerability counts.

## Technology and partnership signals

- Tanger Med and Wärtsilä announced co-development of a next-generation Port Management Information System to improve call management, safety and emissions.
- Tanger Med operates its own PCS spanning port calls, gate access, import/export, track-and-trace and payment workflows.
- The group joined chainPORT and participates in a Tanger Med–Rotterdam digital corridor.
- A 2024 digital-twin proof of concept brought together the Digitalization Team, TMPA Maintenance, Tanger Med Engineering asset management and PHD Soft around the TM2 link bridge.
- Student/project profiles refer to pipelines combining information-security management, SOC and NOC data. This is a capability clue, not proof of production architecture.
- The published digitization strategy explicitly calls for cyber resilience across the port ecosystem and secure data sharing.

## Opportunity designs

### Infrastructure-maintenance evidence

Tanger Med Engineering/TMPA Maintenance uses an approved device during a bridge, utility or facility walkdown. Observations and capture provenance feed the existing asset process. This avoids beginning with safety-critical terminal equipment.

### Operator audit kit

A qualified auditor uses the product for one independently authorized terminal/tenant assessment. Tanger Med site-access rules and the operator's network authorization are both recorded.

### PCS-connected asset reconciliation

Only after a passive benchmark, test whether field observations can link to asset or service records without exposing PCS secrets or creating a new source of truth.

## Product implications

- Legal-entity and network-owner fields must be mandatory.
- The application must distinguish shared infrastructure, TMPA platforms, terminal OT and tenant factories.
- APIs/import-export should be prioritized because Tanger Med already operates an interconnected digital ecosystem.
- Offline operation must still support controlled later synchronization and group data-sovereignty requirements.
- Physical access, radio, camera and USB policies need a visible preflight check.

## Unknowns

- Exact division of responsibility between Group CDO, Cybersecurity Director and TMPA Technology & IT Security.
- Current PMIS/PCS/asset-management architecture and whether portable inventory is a recognized gap.
- Terminal-operator technology stacks and approved audit providers.
- Which noncritical maintenance environment could provide a reference inventory.
- Procurement route through TMPA, Tanger Med Engineering or an existing integrator.
- Whether any PCAP may leave the port and where it may be processed.
