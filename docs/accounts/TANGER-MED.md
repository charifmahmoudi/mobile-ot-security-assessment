# Tanger Med Group

## Account structure

Tanger Med is a group of related operating entities, not a single port IT department. Its published structure covers port and logistics activities, industrial platforms and services. Tanger Med Port Authority (TMPA) manages the port complex and port community; Tanger Med Zones develops and manages industrial zones; terminal operations include separate operators. A sale to “Tanger Med” therefore needs a precise operating entity and network owner.

The group reports a Digitalization Committee among its internal management committees. Its digital strategy explicitly calls for cyber resilience across the value chain and port ecosystem, secure data sharing, drones, AI and blockchain. This is strong strategic alignment, but it also raises the assurance bar for any field device.

## What the organization is doing publicly

- Tanger Med describes an end-to-end digital strategy and a cyber-resilient data-sharing environment.
- It joined the chainPORT network and is participating in a Tanger Med–Rotterdam digital corridor.
- A 2024 proof of concept joined the Digitalization Team, TMPA Maintenance, Tanger Med Engineering asset management and PHD Soft around a bridge digital twin and predictive maintenance.
- Tanger Med hosted the International Port Community Systems Association annual meeting in 2024, where cybersecurity, AI and supply-chain resilience were agenda topics.
- Tangier Logistics Days 2025 focused on digitally connected supply chains and data.

These activities suggest a customer that can evaluate a digital field instrument, but also one that may already have sophisticated platforms. Atlas OT Scout must interoperate rather than become another isolated inventory.

## Account entry thesis

The most credible first case is not container-crane scanning. It is a controlled infrastructure or maintenance walkdown led by TMPA/Tanger Med Engineering: combine imported asset records, passive capture from an approved observation point and physical interface observations, then export a reconciliation package. A second case is portable collection for an authorized audit of a tenant or subsidiary, provided every operator grants permission.

Events offer specific entry routes: IPCSA, Tangier Logistics Days, TOC Africa and Industry Meeting Day. The objective is to identify the owner of a maintenance or cyber-resilience workflow, not simply collect executive contacts.

## What must be checked

- Which legal entity owns the proposed network and capture data.
- Whether port terminals, PCS, physical security and facilities networks are separated under different teams.
- Existing discovery/CMDB platforms and their import APIs.
- Current approved cyber assessment providers and integrators.
- Restrictions on wireless radios, photography, USB and data export in the port zone.
