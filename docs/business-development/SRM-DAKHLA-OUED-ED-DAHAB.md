# SRM Dakhla–Oued Ed-Dahab account dossier

_Research baseline: **1 September 2026**. Qualification research only; no purchasing relationship or system access is implied._

## Account

| Priority | Status | Geography | Water scope |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 — concentrated renewal and non-conventional-water account | Public regional multiservice company; operational since 6 October 2025 | Dakhla–Oued Ed-Dahab region | Regional drinking-water distribution, liquid sanitation and electricity services |

## Verified public evidence

- **Contract and programme:** The launch reporting gives a 30-year contract and about MAD 1.82bn planned investment.
- **Large renewal package:** A 2026 notice covers about MAD 164.7m of water and sanitation renewal in Dakhla neighbourhoods.
- **Non-conventional operations:** A current procurement covers operation of modular demineralisation/desalination stations and associated boreholes.

## Current public signals

- **Concrete plant estate:** Modular treatment stations, boreholes, network renewal and new connections create bounded assessment candidates.
- **Rapid infrastructure change:** Large simultaneous works increase the risk of asset-record drift and handover gaps.
- **Water-source diversity:** Desalination, demineralisation, groundwater and network distribution require clear source-to-customer boundaries.

## Atlas entry wedge

Prioritise one modular treatment station, pumping/storage package or a single renewal lot. Build a commissioning and asset-evidence handoff rather than scanning the city network.

Keep the first engagement to one operator, one process area and one decision. Passive or imported evidence is the default; any packet-producing action requires exact written authorization.

## Buying group and qualification

**Roles to identify:** Water production/distribution interface; treatment-station operations; network-renewal project owner; sanitation operations; asset management/GIS; maintenance contractor; IS/cybersecurity.

**Questions:** Which entity owns and operates each desalination/demineralisation unit? Which renewal lot has a defined acceptance date and asset list? What records exist for boreholes, pumps, treatment skids and telemetry? What legal, contractual and data-handling approvals are required?

**Risks:** Perform legal, contractual and customer-policy review before outreach or field work. Do not assume all desalination or irrigation infrastructure belongs to the SRM. Tender scope and award status must be verified on the official portal.

Public sources do not prove installed vendors, protocols, topology, vulnerabilities, budget availability or permission to test.
