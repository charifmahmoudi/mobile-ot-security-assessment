# Priority account hypotheses

This document contains research hypotheses only. It does not record customer activity, current relationships, assigned work or qualified opportunities.

## Evidence labels

- **Verified public evidence** — the cited source was checked and supports the exact statement shown.
- **Secondary public lead** — a non-primary source points to a potentially relevant public record; the primary record must be checked before external use.
- **Commercial hypothesis** — a proposition to test with a customer; it is not a fact.
- **Unknown** — information that has not been established.

## SRM Fès–Meknès

### Verified public evidence

The official SRM-FM procurement page lists:

- AO 54/2026: supply and installation of drinking-water telemanagement equipment in the Taza provincial directorate;
- AO 98/2026: supply and upgrade of the existing central telemanagement system.

**Research source:** `FM-001` in `research/WEB-SOURCE-REGISTER.csv`.

The notices establish that procurement activity was published. They do not establish award status, implementation status, a handover problem, customer demand or Atlas suitability.

### Commercial hypothesis

A bounded telemanagement project may have a need to reconcile commissioned equipment, approved records and acceptance evidence before handover.

### Unknowns

- award and implementation status;
- relevant project or operational boundary;
- current acceptance method;
- authoritative inventory or as-built record;
- responsible technical, operational and procurement functions;
- consequence of unresolved discrepancies;
- suitable evidence source or representative environment;
- willingness to evaluate or pay.

### Functions that would need to be identified

- project or programme management;
- water operations;
- automation or telemanagement engineering;
- project acceptance or technical review;
- procurement and legal;
- security or data governance.

### Validation question

> For a bounded telemanagement lot, how are commissioned field assets reconciled with the approved inventory and acceptance evidence before operational handover?

### Evidence required to advance

An actual interaction must confirm a bounded problem, its consequence, a timing event and a next step. Until then, the account remains at `Research`.

## SRM de l’Oriental

### Secondary public lead

Public procurement mirrors report field-recognition, restitution and GIS-capture work for SRM de l’Oriental, including water and sanitation lots. The current research record is graded `C` because it relies on mirrors of the official procurement portal.

**Research sources:** `ORI-001` to `ORI-004` in `research/WEB-SOURCE-REGISTER.csv`.

The official notice and current procurement status must be checked before the information is used externally. The existing sources do not establish a data-quality problem, customer demand or Atlas suitability.

### Commercial hypothesis

A bounded field-recognition or asset-data process may require a controlled review of differences between field observations, GIS records and contractor documentation.

### Unknowns

- authoritative official notice and current status;
- relevant water, sanitation or electricity scope;
- authoritative data model and record owner;
- current reconciliation and approval process;
- accountable technical and governance functions;
- operational consequence of incorrect records;
- suitable evidence source;
- willingness to evaluate or pay.

### Functions that would need to be identified

- GIS or asset-data management;
- water or sanitation engineering;
- network operations;
- programme or contract management;
- data governance;
- procurement and legal.

### Validation question

> When a field observation conflicts with the approved GIS, inventory or contractor record, what evidence and review process determine the accepted asset state?

### Evidence required to advance

An actual interaction must confirm a repeatable reconciliation problem, a bounded scope, its consequence and an accountable review process. Until then, the account remains at `Research`.

## OCP Green Water

### Verified public evidence

An official OCP Group water-programme page identifies commissioning of the Safi–Gantour pipeline as a 2026 milestone and describes the Safi desalination plant and associated transfer infrastructure.

**Research sources:** `OCP-002` and `OCP-004` in `research/WEB-SOURCE-REGISTER.csv`.

The public statement establishes a programme milestone. It does not establish a control-system handover problem, a decision process, a buyer or interest in Atlas.

### Commercial hypothesis

A bounded commissioning area may benefit from a reviewed, evidence-linked baseline connecting approved records, observed identities and unresolved discrepancies.

### Unknowns

- exact commissioning gate and current schedule;
- EPC-to-operations handover process;
- authoritative asset and commissioning records;
- existing tools and suppliers;
- operational, technical, security and procurement functions;
- acceptable evidence environment;
- consequence of unresolved discrepancies;
- willingness to evaluate or pay.

### Functions that would need to be identified

- commissioning management;
- operations and maintenance;
- process automation or control engineering;
- project controls or quality assurance;
- security and data governance;
- procurement and legal.

### Validation question

> For one bounded commissioning area, what evidence demonstrates that the accepted asset record corresponds to the equipment and control elements actually delivered?

### Evidence required to advance

An actual interaction must confirm a commissioning-evidence problem, its consequence, a dated decision gate and a credible review route. Until then, the account remains at `Research`.
