## Claims not established by current evidence

- No customer-accepted Atlas product price has been recorded.
- No controlled evidence shows that the phone workflow saves time or money versus the customer's current process.
- No defensible Morocco TAM, SAM or SOM is available from the reviewed public evidence.
- Public tenders and job descriptions do not establish installed protocols or equipment unless the source names them.
- A USB-Ethernet connection alone does not provide visibility into arbitrary third-party traffic on a switched network; passive third-party observation still requires an appropriate SPAN/TAP or equivalent capture path.

Treat these as evidence boundaries in commercial material. Do not convert them into positive claims without new primary evidence.
