## Current public signals

- **Active renewal:** The awarded lots indicate broad water-network work and contractor activity.
- **Coastal/urban diversity:** Tanger, Tétouan, Al Hoceïma and provincial centres have different operating and infrastructure contexts.
- **Transition evidence need:** Regionalisation may require reconciliation among Amendis-era, former régie/ONEE and SRM records.

## Atlas entry wedge

Choose one awarded water-network package or one named sanitation facility after operator confirmation. Position Atlas as a handover and evidence-reconciliation tool, not as a regional scanner.

Keep the first engagement to one operator, one process area and one decision. Passive or imported evidence is the default; any packet-producing action requires exact written authorization.

## Buying group and qualification

**Roles to identify:** SRM provincial water operations; Amendis technical owner where still applicable; project and contractor acceptance; network performance; sanitation operations; asset management/GIS; IS/cybersecurity.

**Questions:** Which entity currently operates and authorizes the exact site? Does the work lot include stations or telemetry, or only civil/network work? What predecessor inventory and as-built records exist? Is there a transition timetable that affects ownership during the pilot?

**Risks:** Do not state that Amendis has exited unless an authoritative current source confirms the exact perimeter. Avoid combining Tanger, Tétouan and Al Hoceïma into one technical scope. Current service indicators are context, not proof of a specific OT system.

Public sources do not prove installed vendors, protocols, topology, vulnerabilities, budget availability or permission to test.
