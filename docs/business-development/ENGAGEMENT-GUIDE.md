# Engagement guide

This guide defines a professional process for preparing and conducting customer validation. It is a template. It does not indicate that any message was sent, any meeting occurred or any person accepted responsibility.

## Preconditions

Before external outreach:

1. recheck the account trigger against a current primary source;
2. verify the recipient's current public role through a legitimate source;
3. confirm that the role is plausibly relevant to the question;
4. identify the exact public fact that may be referenced;
5. remove any statement that implies a relationship, authority, vulnerability or customer problem;
6. select an approved communication channel;
7. confirm that the message requests only a conversation or referral, not system access.

A public profile or procurement notice is not consent to contact, proof of authority or evidence of demand.

## Initial message structure

An initial message should contain four elements:

- the verified public event or project;
- one operational question;
- a clear statement that no production access is requested;
- a request for correction or referral if the recipient is not the appropriate function.

### French template

> Bonjour [Nom],  
> J’ai consulté [source publique exacte] concernant [projet ou événement]. Je cherche à comprendre comment [décision opérationnelle précise] est actuellement vérifiée pour un périmètre limité.  
> Cette demande porte uniquement sur un court échange de validation et ne concerne aucun accès à un réseau de production. Êtes-vous la fonction appropriée, ou pourriez-vous m’indiquer le service concerné ?

Do not present this template as a sent message. Record it as activity only after it is actually transmitted through a known channel.

## Account-specific question examples

These are research prompts, not customer facts.

### SRM Fès–Meknès

> Pour un lot limité de télégestion, comment les équipements mis en service sont-ils rapprochés de l’inventaire approuvé et des preuves de réception avant le transfert à l’exploitation ?

### SRM de l’Oriental

> Lorsqu’une observation terrain contredit le SIG, l’inventaire ou le dossier d’un prestataire, quelles preuves et quel circuit de revue déterminent l’état d’actif accepté ?

### OCP Green Water

> Pour une zone limitée en phase de commissioning, quelles preuves démontrent que l’inventaire accepté correspond aux équipements et éléments de contrôle effectivement livrés ?

## Follow-up control

A follow-up should be considered only when an initial message was actually sent and the communication policy permits it. It should add useful clarification rather than repeat the original request.

### French template

> Bonjour [Nom],  
> Je précise que l’objectif est uniquement d’évaluer, sur un exemple limité, la manière dont l’inventaire attendu, les preuves disponibles et les écarts non résolus sont revus. Il ne s’agit ni d’un scan, ni d’une demande d’accès production.  
> Un court échange avec la fonction responsable de la réception technique ou de la gouvernance des données permettrait de déterminer si ce sujet est pertinent.

Do not set a follow-up date or assign it to a person unless that responsibility has been explicitly accepted outside this document.

## Discovery discussion

A discovery discussion should establish facts, not confirm a predetermined sales narrative.

### Required questions

- What exact decision is difficult?
- How is the decision handled today?
- What evidence is considered authoritative?
- What problem, delay, rework or dispute occurs?
- What is the operational or project consequence?
- Is there a dated event or decision gate?
- What bounded area or artifact could be reviewed safely?
- Which functions review technical evidence?
- Which function controls budget and procurement?
- What existing process, supplier or tool addresses the issue?
- What would make Atlas unsuitable?
- What commercial structure, if any, could be considered?

### Close

Summarize only what the participant stated and ask for one explicit outcome:

- a correction to the hypothesis;
- a referral to the relevant function;
- a review of the synthetic evidence example;
- a second discussion with the required technical function;
- a decision that the topic is not relevant.

## Logging standard

An interaction may be added to `activity-log.csv` only when it actually occurred. The record must include:

- date;
- organisation;
- person or official channel;
- communication channel;
- activity type;
- factual outcome;
- source or evidence reference;
- next step only when one was explicitly agreed.

Do not log internal drafting, browsing, role speculation or planned communication as customer activity.
