# Morocco prospect directory

_Reviewed: 1 September 2026._

This directory identifies organisations and public channels relevant to the first Moroccan market campaign. Company pages, public projects, media items and professional profiles are linked in the text. Only publicly published organisation addresses are included; no personal email address is inferred.

## Priority end users

### ONEE – Branche Eau

**Why it is relevant.** [ONEE – Branche Eau](https://www.onep.org.ma/) plans and operates drinking-water production and supply infrastructure and provides sanitation services for local authorities. Its published [technical clauses](https://www.onep.org.ma/Espace-Achats-ONEE-BO/Cahiers-Clauses-Techniques_dispo-Generales/Cahiers-Clauses-Techniques_dispo-Generales.htm) include telemanagement, automation and regulation systems.

**Marketing angle.** Remote-station baselines, pumping and treatment handover, maintenance evidence, imported packet-capture review, and one bounded regional or project area.

**Public channels.** [ONEE – Branche Eau on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/onep), [official website](https://www.onep.org.ma/) and [contact@onep.ma](mailto:contact@onep.ma), which is listed by the [National Electricity Regulatory Authority](https://anre.ma/liens-utiles/). Public telephone: 05 37 65 06 49.

**Relevant public signal.** ONEE’s technical library uses the same telemanagement, automation, pumping and treatment vocabulary required for a technically credible Atlas message.

### OCP Green Water

**Why it is relevant.** [OCP Green Water](https://www.ocpgroup.ma/en/who-we-are/our-strategic-entities-and-partners) develops and operates non-conventional water infrastructure. OCP’s [current water programme](https://www.ocpgroup.ma/en/taxonomy/term/26) describes desalination, drinking-water supply and transfer systems at large scale.

**Marketing angle.** Commissioning acceptance for one process area, EPC-to-operations handover, evidence ownership, continuity of integrated desalination and transfer systems, and OT network architecture consulting.

**Public channels.** [OCP Green Water on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ocpgreenwater), [OCP Group on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ocpgroup/) and the [OCP Group contact form](https://www.ocpgroup.ma/en/contact-ocp). The OCP Casablanca headquarters telephone published on that page is (+212) 05 22 92 40 00.

**Relevant public people and media.**

- [Ahmed Znibar](https://www.ocpgroup.ma/en/press-release/ocp-green-water-secures-mad-6-billion-usd-620-million-financing-assistance-cdg-group), CEO of OCP Green Water, is quoted in OCP’s announcement of MAD 6 billion financing for water infrastructure. Financing, scale, resilience and delivery are therefore relevant executive themes.
- [Hanane Mourchid](https://www.ocpgroup.ma/en/who-we-are/our-leadership-and-governance), Managing Director in charge of Sustainability & Green Utilities at OCP Group, is the official executive profile connecting water, renewable energy and operational resilience.
- [Abousselham Otmane](https://ma.linkedin.com/in/abousselham-%D8%A3%D8%A8%D9%88%D8%B3%D9%84%D9%87%D8%A7%D9%85-otmane-%D8%B9%D8%AB%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%86-04653618) publishes publicly about desalination, operations and integrated water infrastructure. His public content supports an operations-and-continuity opening rather than a compliance-only message.

No verified individual business email was found in the reviewed official sources.

### SRM Fès–Meknès

**Why it is relevant.** The [SRM-FM website](https://www.srm-fm.ma/) provides the current regional operator channel. Its [official procurement page](https://www.radeef.ma/appel-offres/avis) includes telemanagement equipment and central-system upgrade work, creating a concrete use case for commissioning evidence and remote-station baselines.

**Marketing angle.** Telemanagement acceptance, central-system upgrade, remote-station inventory, inherited records and maintenance baselines.

**Public channels.** [SRM Fès–Meknès on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/soci%C3%A9t%C3%A9-r%C3%A9gionale-multiservices-f%C3%A8s-mekn%C3%A8s-s-a), [contact@srm-fm.ma](mailto:contact@srm-fm.ma) and public telephone 05 35 55 00 00, published on the [operator website](https://www.radeef.ma/).

### SRM L’Oriental

**Why it is relevant.** [SRM L’Oriental](https://www.srm-ori.ma/) manages water, sanitation and electricity services across the region. A public tender listing reported [field-recognition and GIS-capture work](https://www.hubforas.com/ao/1db466e6-1195-4ac6-aefc-33063926d6c0-reconnaissance-terrain-des-reseaux-restitution-et-saisie-dan); the listing is a secondary source, but it identifies a relevant field-to-record marketing theme.

**Marketing angle.** Field-to-GIS reconciliation, evidence for network-data changes, regional asset consolidation and a portable workflow for geographically distributed sites.

**Public channels.** [SRM L’Oriental on LinkedIn](https://ma.linkedin.com/company/srmloriental), [official website](https://www.srm-ori.ma/), [contact@srm-ori.ma](mailto:contact@srm-ori.ma) and public telephone 05 36 743 743. The email and telephone were published in a [CMI service announcement](https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/cmimaroc_%F0%9D%90%88%F0%9D%90%A6%F0%9D%90%A9%F0%9D%90%A8%F0%9D%90%AB%F0%9D%90%AD%F0%9D%90%9A%F0%9D%90%A7%F0%9D%90%AD-%F0%9D%90%80%F0%9D%90%AF%F0%9D%90%A2%F0%9D%90%AC-%F0%9D%90%9A%F0%9D%90%AE%F0%9D%90%B1-%F0%9D%90%A1%F0%9D%90%9A%F0%9D%90%9B%F0%9D%90%A2%F0%9D%90%AD%F0%9D%90%9A%F0%9D%90%A7%F0%9D%90%AD%F0%9D%90%AC-activity-7267472032341049345-x1Kj).

**Relevant public signal.** The company’s LinkedIn feed publishes regional water-service, wastewater-reuse, maintenance and customer-service updates, making it the best current source for operational timing.

### SRM Casablanca–Settat

**Why it is relevant.** The [official creation notice](https://srm.radeec.ma/creation-de-la-srm-cs/) states that SRM Casablanca–Settat assumed services previously managed by Lydec, RADEEJ, RADEEC and ONEE regional branches. This makes predecessor-record consolidation and transition evidence directly relevant.

**Marketing angle.** Operator-transition baselines, project handover, asset-data quality and bounded reviews for pumping, reservoirs or sanitation facilities.

**Public channels.** [SRM Casablanca–Settat on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/srm-casablanca-settat), [official website](https://srm.radeec.ma/), [contact@srm-cs.ma](mailto:contact@srm-cs.ma) and public telephone (+212) 522 31 20 20, published on the [official contact page](https://srm.radeec.ma/contact/).

### SRM Souss-Massa

**Why it is relevant.** Souss-Massa is a high-priority water-security region. Current public investment includes the [Tamri dam project for Grand Agadir](https://www.maroc.ma/fr/actualites/grand-agadir-le-barrage-tamri-un-projet-structurant-pour-garantir-la-securite-hydrique), alongside desalination, irrigation and regional water infrastructure.

**Marketing angle.** Pumping and treatment reliability, drought resilience, non-conventional water interfaces, wastewater reuse and geographically distributed assets.

**Public channels.** [SRM Souss-Massa website](https://www.srm-sm.ma) and [LinkedIn page](https://www.linkedin.com/company/srmsoussmassa). Use the official website contact route; no current generic business-development email was verified for this guide.

### SRM Marrakech–Safi

**Why it is relevant.** The region combines a major tourism centre, rural supply, sanitation, industrial water and reuse interfaces. The [Aït Ziyat dam project](https://www.maroc.ma/fr/actualites/barrage-ait-ziyat-infrastructure-strategique-pour-renforcer-la-securite-hydrique-et-soutenir-le) illustrates the scale of current water-security investment.

**Marketing angle.** Water-project handover, sanitation rehabilitation, reuse interfaces, pumping assets and field-to-record evidence across multiple provinces.

**Public channels.** [SRM Marrakech–Safi on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/srmmarrakech-safi). No current generic email or independently verified official social-media URL beyond LinkedIn was found for this guide.

## Engineering and channel partners

[Aquatech Morocco](https://www.linkedin.com/company/aquatech-services-des-eaux-maroc/) works in water and wastewater operations, leakage, GIS, CMMS and treatment. Atlas can complement its operational and asset-management services with a portable evidence-baseline workflow.
