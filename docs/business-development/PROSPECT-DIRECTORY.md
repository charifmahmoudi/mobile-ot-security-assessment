# Morocco prospect directory

_Reviewed: 1 September 2026._

This directory owns **named-account facts, public project signals and published contact routes** for Morocco business development. It does not own generic positioning or outreach messages; use the [market guide](MOROCCO-MARKET-GUIDE.md) and [marketing playbook](MARKETING-PLAYBOOK.md) for those.

Only publicly published organization contact routes are included. No personal email address is inferred.

## Priority water and infrastructure operators

### ONEE — Branche Eau

**Evidence of relevance:** [ONEE – Branche Eau](https://www.onep.org.ma/) operates drinking-water production/supply and sanitation services. Its published [technical clauses](https://www.onep.org.ma/Espace-Achats-ONEE-BO/Cahiers-Clauses-Techniques_dispo-Generales/Cahiers-Clauses-Techniques_dispo-Generales.htm) include telemanagement, automation and regulation terminology.

**Public channels:** [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/onep), [official website](https://www.onep.org.ma/), `contact@onep.ma` as listed by the [National Electricity Regulatory Authority](https://anre.ma/liens-utiles/), public telephone 05 37 65 06 49.

### OCP Green Water

**Evidence of relevance:** [OCP Green Water](https://www.ocpgroup.ma/en/who-we-are/our-strategic-entities-and-partners) develops/operates non-conventional water infrastructure. OCP's [water programme](https://www.ocpgroup.ma/en/taxonomy/term/26) describes desalination, drinking-water supply and transfer systems.

**Public channels:** [OCP Green Water LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ocpgreenwater), [OCP Group LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ocpgroup/), [OCP contact form](https://www.ocpgroup.ma/en/contact-ocp), public Casablanca headquarters telephone (+212) 05 22 92 40 00.

**Relevant public leadership/content:** [Ahmed Znibar](https://www.ocpgroup.ma/en/press-release/ocp-green-water-secures-mad-6-billion-usd-620-million-financing-assistance-cdg-group), [Hanane Mourchid](https://www.ocpgroup.ma/en/who-we-are/our-leadership-and-governance), and public operations/desalination content from [Abousselham Otmane](https://ma.linkedin.com/in/abousselham-%D8%A3%D8%A8%D9%88%D8%B3%D9%84%D9%87%D8%A7%D9%85-otmane-%D8%B9%D8%AB%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%86-04653618). No verified individual business email was found in the reviewed official sources.

**Evidence of relevance:** [SRM Fès–Meknès](https://www.srm-fm.ma/) is a current regional multiservice operator. Its [procurement page](https://www.radeef.ma/appel-offres/avis) has included telemanagement-related work.

**Evidence of relevance:** regional water-security investment includes the [Tamri dam project for Grand Agadir](https://www.maroc.ma/fr/actualites/grand-agadir-le-barrage-tamri-un-projet-structurant-pour-garantir-la-securite-hydrique), alongside desalination/irrigation infrastructure.

**Public channels:** [official website](https://www.srm-sm.ma), [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/srmsoussmassa). No current generic business-development email was verified for this directory.

**Evidence of relevance:** current water-security investment includes the [Aït Ziyat dam project](https://www.maroc.ma/fr/actualites/barrage-ait-ziyat-infrastructure-strategique-pour-renforcer-la-securite-hydrique-et-soutenir-le), and the region contains water, sanitation, industrial and reuse interfaces.
