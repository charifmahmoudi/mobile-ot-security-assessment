# Morocco target accounts and partners

_Reviewed: 2 September 2026._

This directory is the working account portfolio for Atlas OT Scout in Morocco. It focuses on the people and situations most likely to support a useful technical conversation, evaluation or partnership.

The strongest account is not necessarily the largest organization. Priority increases when there is a current project or operating trigger, a relevant technical stakeholder, a credible sponsor and enough public evidence to approach the account specifically.

## Contact discipline

- Relevance matters more than seniority; a project, automation, GIS or operations lead is often a better first contact than a Director General.
- Current responsibility matters more than historical fit. Historical technical work is useful context but does not make someone a current prospect by itself.
- Use public professional activity to understand vocabulary and responsibilities, not to invent private motivations.
- Reconfirm roles before outreach, particularly within newly formed SRMs.
- Use public organization contact routes when no verified person-level professional route exists.
- Do not infer private email addresses, personal phone numbers or other non-public contact details.
