# Morocco water delivery ecosystem and transition map

_Research baseline: **1 September 2026**._

This map separates current customer accounts from organisations that previously operated, finance, engineer, integrate or deliver parts of Moroccan water infrastructure. Presence in a project, article or tender does not make an organisation the buyer or authorization authority for Atlas OT Scout.

## Legacy and transition boundaries

Morocco's regional multiservice reform is not operationally uniform. Qualify the exact commune, facility, asset owner and contract before outreach or field planning.

| Operator | Publicly evidenced position | Business-development implication |
|---|---|---|
| **Lydec** | Veolia announced an agreement in July 2024 to sell its Lydec stake to SRM Casablanca–Settat; the SRM describes predecessor activities as consolidated into the regional operator | Use Lydec records as predecessor evidence. Route current activity through SRM-CS unless an exact current contract says otherwise |
| **Redal** | SRM-RSK's published figures exclude the Redal delegated-management perimeter, while Redal remains visible in current sanitation procurement | Determine the exact commune and asset boundary before naming the buyer or authorization authority |
| **Amendis** | Amendis continues to publish operating information for Tanger and Tétouan while SRM-TTA awards regional works | Treat the current operator and transition status as a live qualification question for each site |

### Transition qualification rules

- Name the exact commune, facility, process area and legal asset owner.
- Identify the entity operating the asset **today**, not only the regional brand.
- Identify who holds the authoritative inventory, maintenance contract and network-authorization power.
- Ask whether a transition, novation, asset transfer or data migration affects the proposed pilot.
- Keep predecessor and successor evidence separate until the customer validates the mapping.
- Never reuse a legacy operator's diagrams, contacts or technical claims as current facts without permission and confirmation.

A transition can support a bounded asset-governance use case: import predecessor and successor inventories, preserve source dates, collect passive evidence for one area, expose duplicates/missing/conflicting identities, require named review and export an evidence-linked handover delta. It is not a penetration test or an allegation that either operator's data is defective.

## Engagement logic

| Situation | Lead account | Supporting ecosystem route |
|---|---|---|
| Existing operating plant or network | Current operator: ONEE, SRM or OCP Green Water | Maintenance contractor and incumbent integrator after operator approval |
| New project before handover | Project owner/operator | EPC, engineer and integrator as delivery participants |
| Development-financed programme | Operator/project owner | Procurement and safeguard requirements from the financing programme |
| Transition between operators | Current legal operator | Predecessor records and transition team as evidence sources |
| Technology demonstration | Operator lab or training owner | Approved integrator or engineering partner |

## Prohibited inference

Do not infer that an engineering firm, financier, contractor or legacy operator:

- owns the production network;
- can authorize testing;
- uses a particular control-system vendor;
- has a mandate covering the entire account;
- is willing to resell or sponsor Atlas.

These remain qualification questions.
