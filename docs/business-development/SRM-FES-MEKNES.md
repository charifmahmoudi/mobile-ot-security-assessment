## Verified public evidence

- **Visible procurement:** The official procurement page lists water-pressure reduction, sectorisation, telemanagement, leak detection, pumping-equipment maintenance and sanitation projects.
- **Telemanagement signal:** A 2026 notice covers supply and installation of drinking-water telemanagement equipment in Taza.
- **Network-performance signal:** Official notices cover leak detection and physical-loss reduction in Meknès, El Hajeb and Ifrane.

## Current public signals

- **Direct OT relevance:** Telemanagement and operating-equipment procurements create a credible, sourced route into control-system evidence.
- **Evidence-rich acceptance:** Sectorisation, pressure control and network rehabilitation have measurable before/after states.
- **Multi-province estate:** Fès, Meknès, Taza, Taounate, Ifrane, El Hajeb, Sefrou and Boulemane require controlled scope selection.

## Atlas entry wedge

Target one telemanagement, pressure-reduction or sectorisation package. Offer an evidence pack that ties field identity, approved design, commissioning record and unresolved deviations to a single acceptance decision.

Keep the first engagement to one operator, one process area and one decision. Passive or imported evidence is the default; any packet-producing action requires exact written authorization.

## Buying group and qualification

**Roles to identify:** Water operations and provincial direction; telemanagement/automation engineering; network performance and leak reduction; asset management/GIS; project management and acceptance; maintenance; IS/cybersecurity.

**Questions:** Which official tender or awarded project has a near-term acceptance gate? Who owns the telemanagement configuration and evidence package? Can a project test network or controlled station be used? What export format must integrate with the SRM's asset workflow?

**Risks:** Tender details can change; verify the official notice and award before outreach. An equipment procurement does not identify the installed vendor until award and site evidence exist. ONEE upstream production projects are a separate ownership boundary.

Public sources do not prove installed vendors, protocols, topology, vulnerabilities, budget availability or permission to test.
