## Buying group and qualification

**Roles to identify:** Regional operations; provincial water and sanitation; rural service programme; project acceptance; asset management/GIS; information systems; industrial-customer interface where applicable.

**Questions:** Which 2026–2030 package has the clearest evidence gap? Who owns the inherited inventory and migration process? What offline-retention and device-custody rules apply? Can one rural or industrial interface be tested without production disruption?

**Risks:** Do not convert rural-development allocations into assumed technology budgets. Confirm whether each asset is owned by the SRM, ONEE, a commune or the customer. Plan logistics and local support before proposing a field deployment.

Public sources do not prove installed vendors, protocols, topology, vulnerabilities, budget availability or permission to test.
