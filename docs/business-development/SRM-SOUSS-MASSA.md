# SRM Souss–Massa account dossier

_Research baseline: **1 September 2026**. Qualification research only; no purchasing relationship or system access is implied._

## Account

| Priority | Status | Geography | Water scope |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — high-investment regional account | Public regional multiservice company; launched in October 2024 | Souss–Massa region | Regional drinking-water distribution, liquid sanitation and electricity services |

## Verified public evidence

- **Five-year plan:** Public reporting on the board-approved plan gives MAD 8.5bn for 2025–2029.
- **2025 budget:** The reported 2025 investment budget is MAD 3.4bn, including MAD 2.4bn for water and MAD 837m for liquid sanitation.
- **Governance:** The board publicly reviewed launch performance, investment, operating budgets and institutional transformation.

## Current public signals

- **Concentrated near-term spend:** The first-year budget is materially front-loaded, creating project-acceptance and inventory-change pressure.
- **Water-security context:** The region combines urban Agadir, rural supply, tourism and non-conventional-water interfaces.
- **Sanitation modernisation:** The public allocation indicates a sizable wastewater programme alongside drinking-water work.

## Atlas entry wedge

Target a single water-security or wastewater-modernisation project in the Agadir metropolitan area or a provincial centre. A strong first offer is a before/after asset and evidence baseline around a pumping, reservoir, transfer or treatment interface.

Keep the first engagement to one operator, one process area and one decision. Passive or imported evidence is the default; any packet-producing action requires exact written authorization.

## Buying group and qualification

**Roles to identify:** Regional water and sanitation directorates; Agadir or provincial operations; project delivery and acceptance; network performance and leak reduction; asset management/GIS; IS and OT security; procurement and engineering partners.

**Questions:** Which 2025–2029 programme package has an unresolved asset-evidence problem? Where does producer ownership end and SRM distribution ownership begin? Which project has a safe test or commissioning environment? What inventory and GIS standards are required at handover?

**Risks:** Do not assume the SRM owns desalination-production assets. Separate drinking-water, sanitation and electricity scopes. Use board-level investment totals only as portfolio context, not as available budget.

Public sources do not prove installed vendors, protocols, topology, vulnerabilities, budget availability or permission to test.
