_Research baseline: **1 September 2026**. This is an account-selection tool, not a sales forecast._

The current Atlas OT Scout Morocco universe contains **14 named operating organisations**: ONEE Water Branch, all 12 regional multiservice companies, and OCP Green Water. Lydec, Redal and Amendis are maintained as transition intelligence; engineering firms, integrators and development-finance institutions are classified as ecosystem participants rather than customers by default.

| Regional distribution and sanitation | All 12 SRMs | Route to reservoirs, pumping, sewerage, treatment, network performance and regional asset data |
| Industrial non-conventional water | OCP Green Water | Route to desalination, reuse, transfer corridors and industrial project handover |
| Delegated/predecessor operators | Lydec, Redal, Amendis | Transition evidence; current buyer and authorization authority must be confirmed per perimeter |
| Delivery ecosystem | Engineering, EPC, integration and finance participants | Influence or delivery role; not automatically a customer or authorization authority |

## Strongest entry wedges

1. **Project acceptance and handover:** reconcile design/as-built inventory, passive evidence and unresolved identities before a reservoir, pumping station, treatment asset, reuse line or telemanagement package is accepted.
2. **GIS and asset-data migration:** support one controlled field-verification lot without replacing the enterprise GIS.
3. **Telemanagement evidence:** validate one exact control-system asset set in a representative or approved environment.
4. **Operator transition:** reconcile predecessor and successor records for one bounded area.
5. **Non-conventional water:** validate a treatment skid, pumping node or transfer corridor.
6. **Water-loss and sectorisation:** link field asset identity to pressure, district and maintenance evidence without claiming leakage causality from protocol data.

## Qualification gate

An account advances only when:

- the current operator and asset owner are confirmed;
- one project, site or operational problem is named;
- the decision supported by the pilot is explicit;
- a technical reviewer and evidence custodian are identified;
- an approved passive or imported evidence source exists;
- any active identity action has exact written authorization;
- retention and export constraints are agreed;
- the customer accepts that unknowns and limitations remain visible.

Use the [company and people map](COMPANY-AND-PEOPLE-MAP.md) to assemble the buying group and the [engagement playbook](ENGAGEMENT-PLAYBOOK.md) to qualify the opportunity.

## Evidence standard and limits

Source IDs resolve in the [web source register](research/WEB-SOURCE-REGISTER.csv). A source supports only the claim recorded in its row. Public research does **not** establish installed OT vendors, protocols, firmware, topology, vulnerabilities, budget availability, project award status, internal authority or permission to access a system.

Transition and delivery-context rules are maintained in the [ecosystem map](ECOSYSTEM-MAP.md). Return to the [business-development index](README.md).
