# Morocco business-development research data

_Research baseline: **1 September 2026**._

This directory contains the machine-readable evidence behind the [account coverage matrix](../ACCOUNT-COVERAGE-MATRIX.md), company dossiers and public-people map.

## Data sets

- [Web source register](WEB-SOURCE-REGISTER.csv) — official, corporate, press and public-procurement sources mapped to the claims they support
- [Account universe](account-universe.csv) — all 14 current operating organisations, priorities, scope and source IDs
- [Project, supplier and technology signal ledger](PROJECT-SUPPLIER-TECHNOLOGY-LEDGER.csv) — dated project and delivery-context signals
- [Public role and event ledger](EMPLOYEE-EVENT-LEDGER.csv) — public professional and event observations used as research leads

The maintained public-profile list used for outreach research is [employee-contact-map.csv](../employee-contact-map.csv).

## Evidence grades

| Grade | Meaning | Permitted use |
|---|---|---|
| A | Government, operator, corporate or development-finance primary source | Company status, mandate, official figures and announced projects |
| B | Established press reporting attributable official or company statements | Corroborated project, board and investment context |
| C | Public-tender mirror linking or referring to the official procurement portal | Current procurement signal; verify the official notice before action |
| D | Contextual lead or secondary analysis | Research question only |

A source proves only the claim recorded in its row. A tender does not prove award, implementation, installed technology or vulnerability. A public role, profile or article mention does not prove buying authority.
