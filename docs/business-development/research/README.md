# Business-development research archive

This directory contains public-source evidence and research leads. It does not contain customer activity, pipeline advancement, assigned tasks, relationship claims or sales forecasts.

## Canonical research assets

- [Account research notes](ACCOUNT-RESEARCH-NOTES.md) — public signals and explicitly labelled commercial hypotheses
- [Ecosystem and transition context](ECOSYSTEM.md) — operator, predecessor, delivery-participant and financing context
- [Public profile lead pool](PUBLIC-PROFILE-LEADS.csv) — unverified public-profile research only
- [Web source register](WEB-SOURCE-REGISTER.csv) — source IDs, dates, grades and supported claims
- [Public employee-event ledger](EMPLOYEE-EVENT-LEDGER.csv) — raw public observations requiring current verification before use

Commercial state is maintained only in [`../pipeline.csv`](../pipeline.csv). Verified working contacts, when they exist, are maintained only in [`../contacts.csv`](../contacts.csv). Actual interactions, when they occur, are maintained only in [`../activity-log.csv`](../activity-log.csv).

## Evidence grades

| Grade | Meaning | Permitted use |
|---|---|---|
| A | Government, operator, corporate or development-finance primary source | Organisation status, official figures, mandate and announced projects within the source date |
| B | Established reporting attributable to official or company statements | Corroborated context; recheck important claims before external use |
| C | Public-tender mirror referring to an official procurement portal | Research lead only; verify the primary notice before use |
| D | Contextual analysis | Research question only |

## Public-profile safeguards

A public-profile record does not establish:

- current employment or title;
- decision authority;
- consent to contact;
- a customer relationship;
- budget, procurement responsibility or technical approval authority.

Before a person is added to `../contacts.csv`, the current role must be independently verified, the verification date and source must be recorded, and the record must remain neutral about authority until established through evidence.

## Claim discipline

- A source supports only the claim recorded in its row.
- Time-sensitive information must be rechecked before external use.
- Public project activity does not establish customer demand.
- Public information does not authorize access to systems or facilities.
- Missing information remains unknown; it must not be completed through inference.
- Research review, drafting and planning are not customer activities.
