## Qualification questions

- Who legally operates and owns the exact asset today?
- Who owns the authoritative inventory and acceptance record?
- Does an EPC, integrator or maintenance contract control the handover route?
- Is the visible organisation a buyer, influencer, evidence source or predecessor?
- Which party can approve a representative review or field pilot?
- Which procurement or financing condition affects the decision?

Never infer an installed vendor, partnership, reseller relationship, authorization power or current contract from public association alone.
