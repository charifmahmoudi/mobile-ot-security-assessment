# SRM Laâyoune–Sakia El Hamra account dossier

_Research baseline: **1 September 2026**. Qualification research only; no purchasing relationship or system access is implied._

## Account

| Priority | Status | Geography | Water scope |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 — newly launched modernisation programme | Public regional multiservice company; operational since 6 October 2025 | Laâyoune–Sakia El Hamra region | Regional drinking-water distribution, liquid sanitation and electricity services |

## Verified public evidence

- **Contract and programme:** Public reporting describes a 30-year contract and an investment programme of about MAD 2.74bn.
- **Modernisation focus:** The launch statement identifies infrastructure modernisation, network performance, service digitalisation and stronger governance.
- **Upstream context:** ONEE continues to publish regional drinking-water production and supply projects, creating producer/distributor interfaces.

## Current public signals

- **Greenfield governance:** A new operator and digitalisation programme create opportunities to define evidence standards early.
- **Water-security projects:** Regional supply and non-conventional-water projects may add new pumping, storage and transfer assets.
- **Handover exposure:** Transition from prior operators requires explicit asset ownership and record provenance.

## Atlas entry wedge

Target one new or rehabilitated water-supply interface, pumping/storage area or sanitation project. Start with a documentary and lab review before any field deployment.

Keep the first engagement to one operator, one process area and one decision. Passive or imported evidence is the default; any packet-producing action requires exact written authorization.

## Buying group and qualification

**Roles to identify:** Regional water operations; project development and acceptance; asset management/GIS; digital transformation/IS; automation and telemanagement; sanitation operations; ONEE interface owner.

**Questions:** Which current project is owned by ONEE, and which by the SRM? What asset and evidence standards are being adopted during digitalisation? Is there a commissioning or training environment? What legal, contractual and data-handling approvals are required?

**Risks:** Perform legal, contractual and customer-policy review before outreach or field work. Do not infer specific OT technology from modernisation language. Confirm the asset owner and authorizing entity for every site.

Public sources do not prove installed vendors, protocols, topology, vulnerabilities, budget availability or permission to test.
