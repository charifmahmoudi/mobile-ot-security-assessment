# OCP Green Water account dossier

_Research baseline: **1 September 2026**. Qualification research only; no purchasing relationship or system access is implied._

## Account

| Priority | Status | Geography | Water scope |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — strategic industrial water operator | Wholly owned OCP Group subsidiary for non-conventional water | Jorf Lasfar, Safi, Khouribga, Benguerir/Gantour and associated supply corridors | Development, production and commercialisation of desalinated and treated wastewater for OCP operations and nearby communities |

## Verified public evidence

- **Ownership and mandate:** OCP identifies OCP Green Water as a wholly owned subsidiary dedicated to sustainable non-conventional water.
- **Financing:** OCP announced MAD 6bn financing in 2025 to expand desalination capacity, with a stated 2030 objective of 630m m³/year.
- **Operating projects:** OCP reports the >200 km Jorf Lasfar–Khouribga desalinated-water pipeline and treated-wastewater transfer from Marrakech to Benguerir/Gantour.
- **External financing:** OCP/IFC and EBRD programmes support desalination, treated-water and pipeline infrastructure.

## Current public signals

- **Industrial-grade estate:** Long pipelines, desalination plants, treatment interfaces, pumping and storage create a substantial OT and asset-management surface.
- **Community supply:** Capacity also supports nearby cities, creating regulated handoff and service-continuity interfaces.
- **Multi-partner delivery:** OCP works with financiers, engineering entities and public operators, increasing the value of traceable acceptance evidence.

## Atlas entry wedge

Target a project-owned test, commissioning or handover segment—such as a pumping station, transfer node or treatment skid—through OCP Green Water and the appointed engineering/integration team. The value proposition is evidence continuity across owner, EPC, operator and receiving utility.

Keep the first engagement to one operator, one process area and one decision. Passive/imported evidence is the default; any packet-producing action requires exact written authorization.

## Buying group and qualification

**Roles to identify:** OCP Green Water operations; project development and engineering; JESA or the appointed EPC/integrator; industrial cybersecurity and digital; maintenance and reliability; water-quality/process engineering; community-supply interface owner.

**Questions:** Which project has a current commissioning, acceptance or brownfield-integration milestone? Who is the system integrator and who owns the final asset register? Can the first evaluation use a FAT/SAT, lab or isolated skid? Which evidence may be exported and which must stay within OCP custody?

**Risks:** Do not treat OCP Group-wide investment targets as a single account budget. Community water supply does not imply shared network authorization. Industrial security, confidentiality and supplier-access requirements will be stricter than a public demo.

Public sources do not prove installed vendors, protocols, topology, vulnerabilities, budget availability or permission to test.
