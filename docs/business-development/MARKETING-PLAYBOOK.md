# Morocco marketing playbook

This page owns **outreach execution**: who to address, what each role is likely to care about, how to frame the problem, which public signal to reference, which channel to use and how to progress a conversation. Market rationale belongs in [MOROCCO-MARKET-GUIDE.md](MOROCCO-MARKET-GUIDE.md), named account/person intelligence in [PROSPECT-DIRECTORY.md](PROSPECT-DIRECTORY.md), and exact evaluation/adoption terms in [OFFER.md](OFFER.md).

## Marketing objective

Create enough technical trust for a suitable practitioner or operator to discuss one bounded evaluation around a real evidence problem. The objective is not broad awareness of a “cyber scanner”; it is a qualified conversation about commissioning, reconciliation, maintenance or authorized assessment work.

## Ideal conversation

A strong prospect has:

- responsibility for a defined water/industrial OT area or professional assessment/integration workflow;
- a bounded evidence or asset-data question;
- legitimate access to the relevant records/evidence;
- someone able to judge whether the result is useful;
- a valid route for authorization and device/evidence custody;
- a current public trigger that makes the question timely.

Use the [prospect intelligence directory](PROSPECT-DIRECTORY.md) to identify the actual person and public trigger before drafting outreach.

## Buying-group map

A serious evaluation will often involve more than one person. Map the account into these functions:

- **Executive sponsor:** can authorize attention, resources or an evaluation.
- **Technical evaluator:** can judge whether the evidence is correct/useful.
- **Operational owner:** experiences the commissioning/maintenance/reconciliation problem.
- **Security/governance gatekeeper:** controls authorization, device/data handling or method acceptance.
- **Procurement/finance:** decides how a successful evaluation can be acquired.
- **Partner/champion:** auditor, integrator or internal practitioner who can carry the workflow into repeated use.

The prospect directory should record named people against these roles where public evidence supports it.

## Core message framework

### 1. Public trigger

Start with something the person can recognize as legitimate and current: a commissioning program, water-network performance initiative, operator transition, telemanagement project, technical hiring signal, public professional talk or audit/critical-infrastructure activity.

### 2. Decision problem

Ask about one unresolved business/technical decision, not a generic cybersecurity pain:

- which inventory should be accepted;
- how a project package becomes the operating baseline;
- how conflicting field/GIS/CMMS evidence is reconciled;
- how an auditor preserves enough provenance to defend a conclusion;
- how much work is spent manually reconciling evidence before reporting.

### 3. Product role

Atlas helps a practitioner collect or import bounded evidence, preserve provenance, review observations and reconcile them with expected records before handoff.

### 4. Low-risk next step

Invite a technical discussion or bounded evaluation only after the problem is confirmed. Exact commercial terms live in [OFFER.md](OFFER.md).

### 5. Boundary

Do not imply complete network visibility, penetration testing, certification, field qualification or any capability not present in [IMPLEMENTATION.md](../../IMPLEMENTATION.md).

### ONEE

For a technical/project contact:

> J’ai vu que l’ONEE continue d’investir dans des programmes d’équipement et que vos documents techniques utilisent largement la télégestion et l’automatisme. Sur un groupe limité de stations, comment l’équipe valide-t-elle aujourd’hui que l’inventaire attendu, les équipements réellement observés et les dossiers de mise en service décrivent le même périmètre ?

For executive/program contacts, keep Atlas out of the first sentence and ask where evidence quality affects execution/acceptance of modernization work.

### OCP Green Water

For an operations/industrial contact:

> Vos communications publiques insistent sur l’intégration production–transfert–réutilisation et sur des mises en service très rapides. Pour un lot limité, quelles preuves sont aujourd’hui utilisées pour démontrer que l’inventaire accepté par l’exploitation correspond bien à ce qui a été livré et mis en service ?

For an executive sponsor, lead with partner-ecosystem handover quality and reduction of unresolved commissioning questions.

### SRMs

For a new regional operator:

> Dans le cadre de la reprise et de la modernisation des réseaux, lorsqu’un dossier hérité, le SIG, la maintenance et l’observation terrain ne concordent pas, quel processus permet de décider quelle donnée devient la référence acceptée ?

Use the specific regional trigger—water-network performance, telemanagement, investment, field-recognition/GIS or operator transition—from the directory.

### Engineering / EPC / integrator

> Lors d’une réception ou d’un transfert à l’exploitant, pourriez-vous valoriser un livrable complémentaire qui relie chaque écart d’inventaire aux preuves conservées, avec les inconnues clairement séparées ?

### Industrial audit / cybersecurity firm

> Nous cherchons d’abord à faire challenger la méthode par des praticiens. Un paquet de preuves avec provenance, revue humaine, limites de visibilité et une opération active strictement bornée pourrait-il s’intégrer à votre méthodologie d’audit industriel, ou quels contrôles manqueraient ?

## First-contact construction

A first message should usually contain four pieces:

1. **Named context:** one public project/activity/role.
2. **One question:** something the recipient is plausibly responsible for.
3. **One-sentence product description:** only enough to explain why you are asking.
4. **Low-friction CTA:** 15–20 minute technical discussion or review of a synthetic example.

Example:

> Bonjour [Nom],  
> J’ai vu votre travail/publication autour de [signal public précis]. Nous développons Atlas OT Scout, un outil hors ligne pour rapprocher l’inventaire attendu de preuves approuvées sur une zone OT limitée. Comment votre équipe traite-t-elle aujourd’hui [question précise] ? Si le sujet vous concerne, je serais intéressé par votre retour technique sur notre méthode.

Do not make the evaluation offer the opening hook when a technical practitioner is the target. Earn relevance first.

## Channel strategy

### LinkedIn

Use LinkedIn for research and context before outreach:

- read the person's recent public posts/interactions when available;
- identify the vocabulary they use for operations/projects/security;
- note whether they publish about specific programs, commissioning, maintenance, automation or governance;
- engage only where the interaction is genuinely relevant;
- reference the public work, not personal trivia.

Useful content themes:

- why an IP address is not durable asset identity;
- “not observed” versus “absent” in switched OT;
- commissioning evidence and inventory reconciliation;
- passive-first assessment and visibility limitations;
- evidence provenance and analyst review;
- field notes on water/telemanagement networks;
- how to structure an evidence delta for handover.

### Official email/contact forms

Use organization routes from the directory when no verified person-level professional route is available. Write for internal forwarding: identify the target function in the first line (“à l’attention de la direction télégestion / exploitation / cybersécurité…”).

### Procurement/project monitoring

Monitor official sources for:

- télégestion;
- automatisme et régulation;
- supervision / SCADA;
- pompage;
- instrumentation;
- SIG / patrimoine;
- reconnaissance terrain;
- mise à niveau / rénovation;
- dessalement / réutilisation;
- maintenance électromécanique;
- cybersécurité / sécurité des SI industriels.

A notice is a **trigger and vocabulary source**, not evidence that Atlas belongs in that procurement.

### Partner outreach

For auditors, engineering firms and integrators, lead with methodology/deliverable fit and repeatability. A partner is more valuable than a single end-user logo when they can apply the workflow across engagements.

## Objection handling

| Objection | Response direction | Follow-up question |
|---|---|---|
| “We already have SCADA/GIS/CMMS.” | Atlas is evaluated as an evidence/reconciliation layer, not a replacement platform. | “Which system wins when those sources disagree?” |
| “We already use Nmap/Wireshark/OT tools.” | Compare workflows on the same bounded task; do not claim superior discovery. | “Where is the manual work after collection—provenance, reconciliation, review or reporting?” |
| “We cannot connect a new device.” | Start with approved documents/captures where useful; network actions require explicit method/scope. | “Could an offline evidence review still answer part of the decision?” |
| “Why a phone?” | Treat form factor as a deployment hypothesis, not the core value. | “What field form factor is actually permitted and useful in your environment?” |
| “The repository is public.” | The offer distinguishes self-build, prepared appliance and specialist services. | “Would your organization rather self-build or evaluate a prepared device first?” |
| “Is the hardware qualified?” | Answer from `IMPLEMENTATION.md`/compatibility evidence only. | “Would a lab/synthetic evaluation be useful before physical qualification?” |
| “How much time/money does this save?” | Not proven; measure it in the evaluation. | “Which workflow steps should we time against your current method?” |

## Follow-up sequence by evidence

Do not run a generic cadence. Progress only when a new reason exists:

- first contact: public trigger + one question;
- follow-up: send one relevant technical note/synthetic example;
- technical review: ask them to challenge evidence schema/safety/reconciliation;
- evaluation scoping: define one area, decision, owner and success measure;
- post-evaluation: record measured effort, objections, acceptance and procurement path.

If there is no response and no new public trigger, stop rather than sending repeated generic nudges.

## Campaign measurement

Measure progression and evidence quality:

- named relevant people mapped per target account;
- accounts with a current trigger and verified technical contact;
- responses by role/message angle;
- technical-method reviews completed;
- bounded evaluation scopes proposed/accepted;
- partner introductions/referrals;
- measured workflow effort from evaluations;
- objections and reasons for no-go;
- paid or contractually binding outcomes.
