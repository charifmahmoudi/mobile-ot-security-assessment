# Sales and marketing playbook — Morocco

## Commercial objective

Create qualified technical conversations around a **specific OT evidence problem**, then convert the strongest conversations into bounded evaluations or partner-led opportunities.

Atlas should not be marketed as a generic cyber scanner. The strongest opening is a real operational or project question: commissioning, handover, inherited records, telemanagement modernization, maintenance preparation, asset reconciliation or an authorized OT assessment.

## Ideal opportunity profile

A strong opportunity has most of the following:

- a defined water, industrial or professional-assessment context;
- a current project, transition, maintenance or governance trigger;
- a technical stakeholder who can judge evidence quality;
- an operational or project owner who experiences the problem;
- a legitimate route for authorization and evidence custody;
- a bounded question that Atlas can help answer;
- a clear criterion for deciding whether the evaluation was useful.

A large account without these elements is less attractive than a smaller account with a concrete decision and the right people.

## Stakeholder conversations

| Stakeholder | What they are responsible for | Atlas relevance | Useful discovery question |
|---|---|---|---|
| Water / wastewater operations | continuity, service quality, operating baseline | resolving uncertain or conflicting asset evidence in one operating area | “When records and field evidence disagree, what does the team accept as the operating baseline?” |
| Engineering / projects / commissioning | project quality, acceptance and handover | evidence-linked delta before accepting equipment or a package | “What evidence closes the gap between as-built documentation and the state accepted by operations?” |
| Automation / télégestion / SCADA | reliable control-system identities and topology | bounded identity/evidence review without replacing engineering tools | “How do you verify the identity and expected role of equipment during a remote-station or SCADA handover?” |
| Maintenance | intervention readiness and reduced rework | bounded baseline plus explicit unresolved items before work | “Which parts of the asset picture still require manual verification before an intervention?” |
| GIS / patrimoine / asset management | authoritative asset records and change control | evidence for accepting or rejecting proposed record changes | “What happens when GIS, maintenance records and field evidence give different answers?” |
| RSSI / OT security / DSI | authorization, security assurance and evidence handling | passive/imported evidence, exact scope and explicit limitations | “What evidence can be collected safely enough to support an inventory or architecture conclusion?” |
| Audit / GRC / methodology | defensibility, repeatability and retained evidence | provenance, review and bounded collection inside an existing method | “Would this evidence package be reusable inside your assessment methodology?” |

## First-contact strategy

A good first contact is short and specific. It should contain:

1. **Relevant context** — a public project, role, professional activity or current transition.
2. **One credible question** — connected to the recipient's responsibility.
3. **A simple Atlas explanation** — enough to explain why the question is relevant.
4. **A low-friction next step** — usually a short technical discussion or review of a synthetic example.

Avoid opening with a product pitch if the recipient is a technical practitioner. Establish relevance first.

### Example structure

> Bonjour [Nom],  
> J’ai vu votre travail autour de [contexte public précis]. Nous développons Atlas OT Scout, un outil hors ligne qui aide à rapprocher l’inventaire attendu de preuves collectées ou importées sur une zone OT limitée. Comment votre équipe traite-t-elle aujourd’hui [question précise liée au rôle] ? Je serais intéressé par votre retour technique sur la méthode.

## Account-specific angles

### ONEE

**Best angle:** project handover, desalination/production infrastructure, telemanagement or maintenance evidence.

For a project or engineering contact:

> J’ai vu les programmes d’équipement et de développement des capacités d’eau non conventionnelle. Sur un lot ou un groupe de stations limité, comment l’équipe valide-t-elle que l’inventaire attendu, les équipements réellement observés et les dossiers de mise en service décrivent le même périmètre ?

Do not begin with enterprise cybersecurity. Begin with the project or operating decision.

### OCP Green Water

**Best angle:** commissioning-to-operations evidence across desalination, reuse and transfer programs.

> Vos activités publiques couvrent des projets où ingénierie, mise en service et exploitation doivent partager une même base d’équipements. Pour un lot limité, quelles preuves sont utilisées pour démontrer que l’inventaire accepté par l’exploitation correspond bien à ce qui a été livré et mis en service ?

For business-development or executive contacts, position Atlas as a partner/tooling question only after technical fit has been established.

### SRMs

**Best angle:** inherited records, operator transition, GIS/patrimony, maintenance and telemanagement modernization.

> Dans le cadre de la reprise et de la modernisation des réseaux, lorsqu’un dossier hérité, le SIG, la maintenance et l’observation terrain ne concordent pas, quel processus permet de décider quelle donnée devient la référence acceptée ?

Use the regional trigger and named stakeholder from the directory. Avoid sending the same SRM message nationally.

### Engineering / EPC / integrators

**Best angle:** reusable evidence at FAT/SAT, commissioning and client handover.

> Lors d’une réception ou d’un transfert à l’exploitant, serait-il utile d’avoir un livrable complémentaire qui relie chaque écart d’inventaire aux preuves conservées et sépare clairement les points confirmés, conflictuels et non résolus ?

### Industrial-audit / cybersecurity firms

**Best angle:** methodology fit and defensibility.

> Nous cherchons à faire challenger la méthode par des praticiens. Un paquet de preuves avec provenance, revue humaine, limites de visibilité et opérations actives strictement bornées pourrait-il s’intégrer à votre méthodologie d’audit industriel ? Quels contrôles ou éléments de preuve manqueraient ?

## Discovery conversation

The purpose of discovery is to determine whether there is a real workflow problem worth evaluating.

### Situation

- What operational area, project or assessment is involved?
- Which team owns the accepted asset baseline?
- Which systems currently hold the expected records: GIS, CMMS, project files, spreadsheets, SCADA documentation or another source?
- What triggers verification today?

### Current process

- How is field or network evidence collected?
- Who reviews identity claims and inconsistencies?
- How are conflicts between sources resolved?
- What tools are already used?
- What part of the process remains manual?

### Consequence

- What happens when the asset baseline is wrong or unresolved?
- Does it cause repeat visits, delayed acceptance, rework, audit uncertainty or maintenance preparation effort?
- Which uncertainty is acceptable, and which must be resolved?

### Evaluation fit

- Can the problem be bounded to one area or decision?
- Which collection methods are permitted?
- Who must authorize network or device access?
- What evidence can be retained or exported?
- What would make the result useful enough to continue?

Do not manufacture urgency. If the current workflow is already adequate, record that and move on.

## Qualification

An opportunity is ready for evaluation when there is:

- a defined assessment question;
- an operational or project owner;
- a technical evaluator;
- an authorization path;
- a bounded site/process scope;
- an agreed expected-state source where reconciliation is part of the use case;
- clear success measures;
- a responsible device/evidence custodian.

A conversation that produces only general interest is not yet an evaluation opportunity.

## Objection handling

| Objection | Response | Follow-up |
|---|---|---|
| “We already have SCADA/GIS/CMMS.” | Atlas is not positioned as a replacement; it is evaluated where those sources need evidence-backed reconciliation. | “Which source wins when they disagree?” |
| “We already use Nmap, Wireshark or OT tools.” | Compare the complete workflow on the same bounded task rather than claiming superior discovery. | “Where is the manual work after collection—review, provenance, reconciliation or reporting?” |
| “We cannot connect a new device.” | Start from approved documents or captures where that can answer the question; network collection requires explicit authorization. | “Could an offline evidence review still resolve part of the decision?” |
| “Why a phone?” | The form factor is a deployment choice, not the core value proposition. | “Which field form factor is actually permitted and useful?” |
| “The project is open source.” | Organizations can self-build or use prepared hardware and specialist services. | “Would you prefer to self-build or evaluate a prepared device?” |
| “Is the hardware qualified?” | Use only the current compatibility and implementation evidence; do not overstate field readiness. | “Would a controlled technical evaluation be useful before site deployment?” |
| “How much does this save?” | The saving is not assumed; it should be measured against the current workflow. | “Which steps should we time and compare?” |

## Channel strategy

### LinkedIn

Use LinkedIn primarily for research, context and selective professional outreach.

- Read current public activity before contacting the person.
- Use the terminology they use for projects, operations, security or engineering.
- Reference only relevant professional activity.
- Do not use personal trivia or inferred contact data.

Useful content themes include:

- expected versus observed asset state;
- why “not observed” is different from “absent” in switched OT;
- commissioning and handover evidence;
- passive-first collection and visibility limits;
- evidence provenance and analyst review;
- reconciling GIS/patrimony and field evidence;
- bounded active identity in sensitive environments.

### Official email and organization routes

When no verified person-level professional route exists, use the published organization channel and make the intended function explicit in the first line, for example “à l’attention de la direction télégestion / exploitation / cybersécurité.”

### Procurement and project monitoring

Monitor public sources for terms such as:

- télégestion;
- automatisme / régulation;
- supervision / SCADA;
- pompage;
- instrumentation;
- SIG / patrimoine;
- reconnaissance terrain;
- rénovation / mise à niveau;
- dessalement / réutilisation;
- maintenance électromécanique;
- cybersécurité des SI industriels.

Use a procurement notice as context and vocabulary, not as evidence that Atlas belongs in that procurement.

### Partner development

With auditors, engineering firms and integrators, lead with **repeatability**: can Atlas become a standard evidence package used across engagements or handovers? Partner opportunities should be evaluated on their potential for repeated use, not only on referral potential.

## Opportunity progression

A practical sales progression is:

**Account research → technical conversation → problem qualification → method review → bounded evaluation → results review → adoption/partner decision.**

At each stage, advance only when there is new evidence of fit.

### Technical conversation

Goal: confirm the problem exists and identify the real owner/evaluator.

### Method review

Goal: expose Atlas's evidence, authorization, reconciliation and limitation model to technical challenge before site use.

### Evaluation scoping

Goal: define one question, one bounded area, one owner, permitted methods and measurable success criteria.

### Results review

Goal: compare Atlas with the current method and decide whether it should be adopted, modified, partner-led or stopped.

## Commercial metrics

Track evidence of progression rather than vanity activity:

- priority accounts with a verified technical stakeholder;
- response rate by stakeholder type and use case;
- technical/method reviews completed;
- qualified evaluation opportunities;
- evaluations started and completed;
- partner-led opportunities;
- measured preparation, field, reconciliation and reporting effort;
- reasons for no-go;
- progression to paid hardware, setup, support or consulting.
