# Morocco water-utility engagement playbook

_Research baseline: **1 September 2026**._

## Positioning

Atlas OT Scout is a guided field-evidence instrument for one bounded water or wastewater assessment. It helps an authorized team reconcile an inventory, preserve provenance and expose what remains unknown. It is not a general scanner, exploitation platform, certification audit or continuous-monitoring replacement.

The commercial model is try-before-buy:

1. select one named process area and decision;
2. review a redacted artifact, representative topology or isolated demonstration;
3. agree authorization, evidence custody, retention and stop conditions;
4. run a passive-first controlled pilot;
5. leave the appliance with the customer for evaluation;
6. the customer acquires it if useful, or Atlas retrieves it if not;
7. consulting and support remain optional.

## Lead use cases

- treatment-plant or pumping-station inventory reconciliation;
- reservoir and remote-station telemanagement baseline;
- district-metering or flowmeter commissioning evidence;
- leak-reduction or sectorisation project handover;
- wastewater-plant, lift-station or collection-network acceptance;
- contractor-to-operator documentation reconciliation;
- GIS/asset-data migration for one field-verification lot;
- pre-audit evidence preparation for one process area.

## Contact sequence

1. **Confirm the operator and asset owner.** Name the region, commune, facility, producer/distributor boundary and any delegated operator.
2. **Anchor the request in a public signal.** Refer to a named project, GIS programme, telemanagement package, treatment asset, sectorisation programme or handover.
3. **Ask for the functional owner.** Seek water operations, sanitation, automation/telemanagement, maintenance/asset management, project acceptance, information systems or procurement.
4. **Offer a document or lab review first.** Use a redacted inventory, sample evidence package, FAT/SAT record or representative capture.
5. **Define one decision.** Examples: accept a contractor handover, reconcile an inventory, verify one controller identity or document report-readiness blockers.
6. **Agree data governance and authorization.** Only then discuss a field pilot.

## Discovery questions

- Who legally operates this exact asset today?
- What decision must the pilot support: commissioning, maintenance, audit preparation, transition or inventory cleanup?
- Which inventory, GIS, topology or contractor record is authoritative?
- Can the team provide an approved capture or representative lab environment before any connection?
- Which protocols and vendors are documented rather than guessed?
- Who reviews operations, automation, security, evidence retention and project acceptance?
- What project milestone or decision date creates urgency?
- What conditions stop the assessment immediately?

## Qualification score

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Water-sector fit | Not water | Mixed or unclear | Explicit water or wastewater |
| Bounded pilot | Regional estate only | Candidate area | One named process area |
| Evidence access | None | Possible | Approved artifact or lab |
| Technical owner | Unknown | Role identified | Named and confirmed |
| Safety acceptance | Broad scanning expected | Undecided | Passive-first accepted |
| Project timing | No event | General modernisation | Current handover, maintenance or acceptance event |

Do not promote an account to pilot status below **9/12**, or when water-sector fit, bounded pilot or safety acceptance scores zero.

## Pilot stages

1. **Scope:** name the site, process area, operator, reviewers and prohibited actions.
2. **Prepare:** import the approved inventory and topology.
3. **Collect:** passive/imported evidence first; use receive-only capture only where authorized.
4. **Review:** accept, reject, merge or leave every observation unresolved.
5. **Close one gap:** optionally execute one exact identity request with a signed, expiring grant.
6. **Handoff:** deliver evidence-linked changes, limitations and blockers.
7. **Evaluate:** measure analyst time, reviewer effort, inventory deltas, blocked unsafe actions and willingness to retain the appliance.

The first commercial proof is a jointly reviewed before/after inventory and defensible evidence package—not a claim that Atlas discovers every device.

## Opening message

> We are validating a passive-first field workflow that preserves evidence while reconciling an authorized water or wastewater asset inventory. Your public programme for **[specific project]** appears to create a project-acceptance or handover question. We are seeking the functional owner of one bounded area who can review a redacted evidence package or an isolated demonstration. We are not requesting production access or proposing a broad network scan.

## Safe language

Say:

- “reconcile one authorized inventory”;
- “observe an approved evidence source”;
- “review one project handover”;
- “document remaining uncertainty.”

Do not say:

- “scan the plant”;
- “find vulnerabilities”;
- “prove security”;
- “discover the whole estate.”

Do not scrape or infer personal contact details, reuse tender contacts for unrelated marketing, imply that a public project reveals a weakness, or ask a visible employee to authorize production testing without the formal operator process.
